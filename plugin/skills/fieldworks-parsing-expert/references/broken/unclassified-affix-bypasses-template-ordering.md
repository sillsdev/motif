## Mechanism

An unclassified affix, and some underspecified inflectional analyses without a slot, load as partial rules. HC permits partial morphology in contexts where a fully classified rule after final inflection would be refused.

## Check and repair hypothesis

Check MSA kind, required category and slot assignment before rearranging a template. Classify from the intended distribution, not from a desire to reduce parse counts.

Test inside/outside the intended template and on a contrasting category, preserving any justified derivation outside inflection.
