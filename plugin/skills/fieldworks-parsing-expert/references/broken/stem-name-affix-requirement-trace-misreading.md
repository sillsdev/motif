## Mechanism

A required stem name can be unchecked while rules are unapplied and enforced only when the candidate is resynthesized. Seeing the reverse rule succeed therefore does not establish that the restriction was ignored.

## Check and repair hypothesis

Follow that same candidate through synthesis and its stem-name rejection or acceptance. Compare one explicitly matching feature bundle with an unmarked bundle.

The reverse trace may look similar; complete confirmation should distinguish the two bundles.
