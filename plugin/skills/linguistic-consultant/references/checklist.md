# Evaluation checklist

**Scope:** FieldWorks 9 family; FLEx 9.3 WinForms terminology baseline from the September design.
Help branch: `sillsdev/FwHelps:markdown-export`, no release tag verified here.
Model vocabulary: `SIL.LCModel 11.0.0-beta0182`. This reference promises no current screen layout.

These sixteen checks turn a broad linguistic judgment into inspectable claims. They are Motif’s
operational synthesis of the cited sources and design, not a validated psychological instrument.

| ID | Check | Evidence required when applicable |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | User and job | Identify the intended user’s need and the job the recommendation serves. |
| C02 | Language evidence | Separate observed forms and contextual readings from hypotheses; preserve provenance. |
| C03 | Terminology | Use the user’s/FLEx terms, explaining necessary MSA or engine abbreviations. |
| C04 | Identity and scope | Bind statements to the supplied project and exact objects; never invent identities. |
| C05 | Affix meaning | State the grammatical contrast and distribution before choosing inflection or derivation. |
| C06 | MSA and category | Check the relevant MSA, category and sense link instead of treating an affix string as a rule. |
| C07 | Slots and templates | Check template participation, declared slot order and required versus optional marking. |
| C08 | Classes and features | Distinguish arbitrary lexical conditioning from semantic/agreement features and sound environments. |
| C09 | Parser reach | Ground engine claims in the pinned importer and parser evidence; saving alone proves nothing about arrival. |
| C10 | Positive readings | Preserve every distinct approved morphology within the declared scope; one reading does not cover all. |
| C11 | Negative evidence | Use Reviewed negatives or a declared universe; unattested forms alone do not prove over-generation. |
| C12 | Completion | Keep unfinished, skipped or unassessed searches distinct from demonstrated absence. |
| C13 | Proportionate change | State parsimony and restrictiveness separately; check regressions and accept alternative valid solutions. |
| C14 | Uncertainty and escalation | Name what is unknown and ask a discriminating question; a person settles linguistic disputes. |
| C15 | Authority and review | Respect project ownership, human Apply and evidence binding to content and Baseline. |
| C16 | Usable explanation | Lead with the action/question, one concrete example, evidence and limits in plain language. |

For each applicable check report `met`, `not_met`, or `insufficient_evidence` with a source locator
or exact answer quote. Use `not_applicable` with a reason for other checks. General writing preferences
do not fail an answer unless the task key makes them required. A task’s atomic required criteria
and explicit prohibitions determine its decision; checklist coverage guides their construction.

A missing required statement is `not_met` with no fabricated quote. A truncated evidence packet is
`insufficient_evidence` and requires repair; missing information deliberately built into an ask-the-user
task is evidence for the expected targeted question. Conflicting claims do not earn credit merely
because a correct phrase also appears. Evidence of authority violations is checked even in polished answers.
