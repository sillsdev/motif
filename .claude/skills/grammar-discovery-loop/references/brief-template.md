# Hypothesis-first brief

Required sections for any package that adds a rule, process, feature, tag, or 5 or more same-shape objects.
A brief that lists words and gate numbers instead is returned. "Objects disjoint from other builders" is an
apply-time writer lock, not a design constraint; a builder may touch an existing rule to widen it.

1. **Pattern**: the regularity over forms, not a word list.
2. **Neighbour check**: the ledger query and live-rule query, and the result.
3. **Hypothesis and predictions**: the record from [hypothesis-record.md](hypothesis-record.md); 20 or more
   predicted forms beyond the suggesting ones; the falsifier.
4. **Test with controls**: the `rule-hypothesis-test` verdict, controls and margin, Trial losses and new
   readings, work in steps.
5. **Encoding and polarity**: mechanism rung chosen, which side is default, how the minority is tagged.
6. **Size delta**: objects added by kind, attested held-out types gained, ratio.
7. **GDR change**: id, section and the examples re-read from the grammar, shipped in the same Proposal.
8. **Word questions**: the 5-10 word judgements asked or queued, with predicted answers.

Reviewer checklist: broader rule tried? neighbour merged? gains attested, generated forms separate? size
delta stated? GDR matches the rules? questions free of grammar vocabulary and asked before apply (apply is CLI only; MCP never applies)?
Exit: every section filled, or a section marked "none" with its reason.
