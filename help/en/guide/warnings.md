# Warnings

**Warnings** shows findings from a check of the whole grammar. They are stored for the [Baseline](term:baseline), and do not depend on which texts or words you selected. Findings do not block an Assessment.

If the grammar has not been checked for this Baseline, choose **Check the grammar**. The parser reads the whole grammar, so a first check can take a minute for a large project. Later visits use the stored findings until the Baseline changes. The page does not check automatically when you open it.

Use **Where…** to narrow findings by where they apply, or **Problem…** to search by the problem described. Select a finding group to read **What it means** and **What usually helps**. A warning describes what the parser reported; use your linguistic judgment to decide what to investigate or change.
