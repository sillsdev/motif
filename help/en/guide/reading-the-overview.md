# Reading the Overview

The **Overview** brings together the results Motif has already stored. The top of the page shows the project, the number of words in the Selection, text occurrences, and counts of wordforms, rules, and lexemes. Its cards summarize **Text Coverage**, **Accuracy**, **Timing**, and **Warnings**.

Text Coverage reports how many selected words and text occurrences parsed. Accuracy describes whether approved analyses were kept. Timing summarizes how long parsing took, including a median and slow words. Warnings are findings from a grammar check; they are about the grammar, not a count of errors in the selected texts. These numbers answer different questions, so read the labels and breakdowns rather than comparing the headline values as if they were one score.

If FieldWorks has saved changes since Motif’s [Baseline](term:baseline), the window marks the stored results as stale. Press **Refresh** to capture the saved project again and measure the current [Default Selection](term:default-selection). Refresh is an explicit action; simply reopening the window does not rerun the parser.

Open **Project history** near the bottom of the page to see recorded activity. Choose a card’s link, such as **Texts →**, **Timing →**, or **Warnings →**, to inspect that information on its page.
