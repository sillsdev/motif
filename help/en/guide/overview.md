# Overview

**Overview** is the first page when you open a project. It gathers stored results and project counts so you can see where things stand without starting a new run.

The top card shows the project and its [Baseline](term:baseline). Other cards summarize **Text Coverage**, **Accuracy**, **Timing**, and **Warnings**. Text Coverage includes parsed words and occurrences; Accuracy compares parser readings with approved analyses; Timing shows how long parsing took; Warnings are findings from a grammar check. These are separate measures, not one overall score.

If FieldWorks has saved since the Baseline, the Overview marks its numbers as stale. Press **Refresh** when you are ready to capture the saved project and assess the [Default Selection](term:default-selection). Use the card links to open **Texts**, **Timing**, or **Warnings** for more detail. Expand **Project history** to see recorded activity.
