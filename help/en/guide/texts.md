# Texts

**Texts** is where you choose material to measure and compare word analyses. Its tabs are **Matrix**, **Analyze texts**, **Lists**, and **What changed**. In **Analyze texts**, use **Read text** to follow words in context or **Word list** to inspect words directly. Choose texts or added words there, then use **Parse all words** to record results for that Selection.

The Matrix groups words by what FieldWorks holds, down the side, and what PanGloss found, across the top: **Same**, **Different**, **No parse**, **Stopped** or **Not parsed**. Each cell says in a few words what happened, such as **Kept**, **Lost** or **Built anyway**. Select a cell to list its words, and choose **Open in text** to see a word in **Analyze texts**. For ticked words, the Matrix offers **Add as Unknown** and **Incorrect spelling**. Approving, disapproving or making an analysis Unknown happens one analysis at a time, in **Analyze texts**. Each action applies to the word form everywhere in the project, not only the occurrence currently shown.

**Lists** asks the Matrix's questions by name, such as **Approved, not parsed** or **Disapproved but built**, and opens on the first list that has words. Tick words to hand off just those, or choose **Hand off the whole list**, to take them to **AI Handoff**.

Changes you collect remain pending until you review and apply them. The page’s **Review changes** link opens that list; it does not write anything to FieldWorks. [Text Coverage](term:text-coverage) appears on the Overview after a measurement.
