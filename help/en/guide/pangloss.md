# PanGloss

On Analyze texts, PanGloss runs the project's grammar on selected texts and compares its readings with what FieldWorks stores.

A means Approved, U means Unknown, D means Disapproved, and a dashed mark means FieldWorks has no reading stored. Blue means PanGloss found a new or different reading.
