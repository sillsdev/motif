# Text Coverage

[Text Coverage](term:text-coverage) shows what share of the Default Selection’s words, and their occurrences in the chosen Texts, parsed in a stored Assessment. The Overview reports both selected words and their occurrences, so the two numbers may differ: one word form can appear many times in a Text.

Use the breakdown to see parsed words alongside words with no parse, incomplete searches, or other recorded outcomes. A word that reached a time or step limit did not complete its search, so read that outcome separately from a completed search with no parse.

Text Coverage is about the selected Texts and words. It is not the same as **Accuracy**, which compares parser readings with approved analyses, or grammar coverage over an outside Corpus. Choose **Texts** to inspect words and occurrences; use [Assessment](term:assessment) to understand which run the Overview is showing.
