# A plural that forgets vowel harmony

Turkish plurals are *-ler* after front vowels and *-lar* after back vowels. The computer knows only what the grammar tells it — so if the grammar gets one condition wrong, whole groups of words stop parsing. Here you'll find which, and why.

## Read the Overview

Text Coverage tells you what share of the words in your texts parse. Note the number — you'll come back to it.

> Screenshot slot: the Turkish sample's Overview with Text Coverage highlighted.

## Find a word that fails

On Texts, sort by status and pick a plural that does not parse — for example *kitaplar*, “books”.

> Screenshot slot: the Turkish sample's Texts page with a failing word selected.

## Ask why

Open Try a Word and type the word. Motif shows how far the parse got and where it stopped: the plural suffix was never allowed after a back vowel.

> Screenshot slot: Try a Word showing where the parse stopped.

## Fix it in FieldWorks, then Refresh

In FieldWorks, open the plural suffix and correct the environment on its *-lar* allomorph. Save, return to Motif, and press Refresh.
