# Corpora and word evidence

A word list alone cannot express the context, human judgement, expected analysis and parser evidence needed to assess grammar changes. Keep the source material, its segmentation, a person's judgement and the result of a particular parser run distinct; a change in one does not silently rewrite the others.

The 2026-07-28 corpus research proposed treating positive and negative examples, ambiguity and regressions as reviewable evidence with provenance and revision history. It was research, not an approved storage design. Current Source identity, occurrence judgements, qualifications and scripture ownership are specified in ADR 0024; assess only comparable scope and analysis under ADR 0020. Context is evidence rather than permission to copy project data into Motif.
