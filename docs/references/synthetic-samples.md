# Synthetic sample provenance

The former research investigated source material for teaching fixtures. Motif retains provenance, not selected source data, extraction scripts or a reconstruction manifest. The separate source licences and any attribution conditions still apply to later use.

- Bantu-style research examined [UniMorph Swahili](https://github.com/unimorph/swc/tree/02a2bdec0e5cb0dc93b6ef11db4d54a82c34b224) at `02a2bdec0e5cb0dc93b6ef11db4d54a82c34b224` (CC BY-SA 3.0), FieldWorks Bantu guidance and published reference works.
- Philippine-style research examined [UniMorph Tagalog](https://github.com/UniMorph/tgl/tree/799d79de338d1370ebfbeabb9823a0e8f86a2b6a) at `799d79de338d1370ebfbeabb9823a0e8f86a2b6a` (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- Turkic-style research examined [UniMorph Turkish](https://github.com/unimorph/tur/tree/6c179ace7d2f3d7f3484020e5304c1544d07bb6b) at `6c179ace7d2f3d7f3484020e5304c1544d07bb6b` (CC BY-SA 3.0). The UD Turkish-IMST treebank was screened out for its non-commercial licence.
- The research consulted Wiktionary and Tatoeba on 2026-09-26; their content carries separate attribution and share-alike terms. These references do not establish speaker approval or claim that any fixture describes a language.
