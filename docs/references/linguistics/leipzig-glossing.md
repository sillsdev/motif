## Summary

Conventions align morphemes and glosses and distinguish word boundaries, segmentation and category labels. Glossing encodes an analysis, which can differ between researchers.

## Short excerpt

From preamble:

> Glosses are part of the analysis, not part of the data.

## Use and limits

Keep observed forms, segmentation, gloss and translation distinct; do not treat a gloss as proof of one grammatical model.
