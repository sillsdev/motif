# Motif 0.2.0-beta001

Motif helps you see which words your FieldWorks grammar can parse, inspect why a word did not parse, and collect changes to analyses before applying them to your project. This beta is for invited testers, with no public announcement. Motif is a working demonstration: keep a FieldWorks backup and try it on a copy of your project first.

## What you can do

- Choose Texts and added words to measure, then read **Text Coverage**, **Approved analyses kept**, timing and grammar warnings on the **Overview**. New choices use a limit of 200,000 parser steps per word; existing choices keep their saved limit.
- Work through a Text from the keyboard, open a word card, and stage **Approved**, **Disapproved** or **Unknown** opinions. **Review changes** shows what will happen before **Apply to FieldWorks project** writes it.
- Use **Try a Word** to read a recorded parser trace, in Plain or Expert view. Grammar warnings show PanGloss's advice, and timing shows the measured share of work, including **Not attributed**.
- Inspect grammar objects and their uses, and open the relevant place in FieldWorks from links that identify it precisely.
- Prepare an **AI Handoff** folder with project context and a question for a chat model. You decide which files to share; Motif does not send them.
- Read progressively loaded Texts and lists, with consistent counts and word cards across the pages.
- When something goes wrong, review **Report a problem** before copying it or opening an issue form. The default report excludes project names and paths, words, grammar and raw parser output. Optional local details may contain private data.

The Windows Setup includes Motif, its command-line tools, the matching PanGloss parser and native dependencies. Download it from this release; no developer checkout or .NET SDK is needed. Linux x64 AppImage and macOS arm64 portable ZIP downloads are previews, built and checked on their respective systems. They do not establish compatibility with every Linux distribution or macOS installation.

## Limits to know before trying it

- Motif reads the saved FieldWorks project. Save in FieldWorks, then use **Refresh** and **Parse all words** to measure your latest edits. Close FieldWorks before applying changes; Motif refuses Apply while FieldWorks owns the project.
- Motif writes nothing to the FieldWorks project until Apply. Selections, measurements and changes not applied yet are stored separately by Motif.
- Motif is pre-1.0. An incompatible Motif store is refused rather than upgraded. **Delete this file and reopen** recreates a refused project store and loses changes not applied yet. A damaged store has manual recovery instructions. These actions do not delete your FieldWorks project.
- Keep an existing workspace at its original project path. Its `.motif.db` file alone is not a complete backup: Baselines and parser artifacts also live under the Motif worker root.
- A word that reaches its step limit has an incomplete search. Finding an analysis does not mean that the search finished. Removing the limit can make difficult words take a long time; **Cancel** remains available.
- Parser support and warnings come from the bundled PanGloss version. A grammar pattern that is unsupported or cannot be measured is not evidence that the word cannot parse.
- An AI Handoff and optional diagnostic details can contain real language data. Review them before sharing. Motif keeps a local usage log of command and argument shapes; it has no usage-upload feature. Writing-system loading may make online language-tag lookups.
- Automatic updates are not enabled. Install the newer release explicitly. Uninstalling Motif leaves your FieldWorks projects and Motif data in place.

See the [install guide](https://github.com/sillsdev/motif/blob/v0.2.0-beta001/src/SIL.Motif.Help/Content/en/guide/install.md) and the Help included in Motif for the first run, keyboard shortcuts and recovery instructions.
