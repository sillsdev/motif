# Window decisions

These decisions preserve the current window choices and record the work that belongs with Proposals.

- The window has five pages (Progress, Texts, Words, Grammar Guide, Proposals), optional Diagnostics and Settings.
  The pages that came before them (Overview, Analyze Texts, Lists, Review Changes, Timing, AI Handoff) are gone, and
  nothing in Help, walkthroughs or the pictures names them.
- Check These Changes, in a Proposal's document, checks for a lost approved reading on words shared by the two
  Assessments and compares parse coverage over those shared words. When the word sets are equal, a new failing word
  that dilutes parse coverage is a regression. When they share no words, the window says the check could not compare.
- A Proposal's before-and-after evidence lives in its own document on Proposals, beside its Rationale and Comments.
