# ADR 0067 — Proposal Differences use captured FieldWorks decisions, human or source-validated

People trying a grammar change can see which words still match an approved analysis and which acquire readings worth checking. Every front end shows the same evidence from the human decisions saved in FieldWorks, or, when asked, from decisions a named program staged from cited sources and always labelled as not a person's. Motif keeps a small summary of each comparison for as long as its Proposal exists, and drops the word-by-word detail as soon as the Proposal is decided or deferred, because that detail is large and only useful while the person is still deciding. A Proposal can hold one comparison for each Text and each set of decisions, side by side, and every front end says which Text and decisions a comparison belongs to.

**Status:** accepted on 2026-10-31; amended on 2026-11-01 (store schema 59: one Difference per Proposal, Text and reference, and word detail also dropped on Defer; word rows stored only for changed or categorised words, at most 100 per cell and category, and 10 places per word). The owner settled the points the first draft left open: a Difference is a standalone result and not an Assessment kind; it carries what it needs by value and no longer keeps its Trial's Assessments alive; the older `Difference` Assessment stays as it is; correct spelling, Wordform ids and writing-system bindings stay unrecorded; and the retention rule below. Wider scope and any later stored shape remain director decisions.

## Decision

A comparison records which texts' human decisions count and the saved Baseline that supplied them. Its summary and detailed pages describe one frozen result, while its findings advise the person without changing permission to Apply.

1. A **Difference** is a saved comparison kept in its own tables. It is not an Assessment kind and not an Assessment row: it cites its Trial's before and after Assessments by id and is complete without them. No separate external annotation store or answer-key role is introduced. The older `Difference` Assessment written by `motif_difference` stays working and is not extended; documents call it the **Trial summary** (CONTEXT). Each word carries one settled state code on the wire: `approved-unique`, `approved-with-alternatives`, `no-approved-match`, `no-parse`, `no-approved-expectations` and `conflicting-decisions`. Three more values say why a state could not be settled: `incomplete` (the search was capped or timed out), `unavailable` (the parser refused, or evidence was invalid or its projection failed) and `not-measured` (no parser evidence was taken). The change-only codes `gained`, `lost`, `changed`, `reading-added` and `ambiguity-up` describe a word with no human decision by comparing the two checkpoints. `reading-added` is a word that kept every reading it had and gained at least one, so it is not labelled `changed`, yet it stays in the `delta.analysis-changed` category and is counted and listed with it, because an added reading can be a wrong parse; `changed` still means a reading was lost or replaced. The stored facts are JSON, so the new code needs no store schema change. The `delta.*` bucket codes are stable wire identifiers, kept as requested; they are not vocabulary.
2. Read approved/disapproved analyses through LibLCM's default-human Opinion properties, spelling through WfiWordform.SpellingStatus, and only explicitly supported human judgments through ADR 0056's saved project records. Unknown is not Disapproved; Correct spelling is not an approved analysis; a disapproved reading is not a forbidden whole word.
3. Freeze what the comparison read, by value: the Text id and title, the Baseline token, the policy, the Proposal's intent digest, each side's Assessor, parser version and hash, grammar hash, Selection name and hash and tokeniser, the occurrence ledger and the per-word facts, for the words the Difference lists (see "Stored rows and their bound"; the other words are only counted in the summary, and only a listed word's first places are kept). The per-word facts include the human-decision witnesses (matched, missing and disapproved analysis ids). Both parser checkpoints use the same captured decisions. Candidate Opinion changes remain separate effects and cannot improve their own agreement score. Changed decisions require a new capture and result. **Not recorded:** correct spelling (a wordform is read as incorrect or undecided), Wordform ids and writing-system bindings. Recording them needs a change to the Baseline capture, not to the Difference.
4. Agreement uses ADR 0027's ordered Form/MSA/InflType identities and guessed-string rule. Lemma, segmentation, POS, class and gloss explain exact objects but never guess identity or broaden approval agreement. Current native morphology is sufficient; no surface segmentation extension or label scorer is required for this feature.
5. On complete decidable evidence show no parse, no Approved match, an Approved match with alternatives, or one Approved match. Keep expected/matched/missing Approved counts, returned Disapproved identities and spelling as independent axes. Finding one approved reading never hides another missing one. Incomplete searches cannot establish absence, uniqueness or rejection; observed forbidden parses remain counterexamples.
6. Store matrices, counts, input/policy bindings and classified word rows in the paired workflow database under ADR 0041. Human decisions remain project data under ADR 0056. A Difference's word detail is derived data and a new store generation drops it (ADR 0077). Its summary is part of its Proposal's record and cannot be produced again once the Proposal is decided, so a store upgrade keeps the summary of every decided Proposal's Difference, marked as having no word detail, and drops the rest; an undecided Proposal's Difference is produced again from a Trial. Migrations keep Proposals and their Difference summaries, and drop or recompute the rest. Difference summaries start with store schema 56: an upgrade from an earlier schema carries none, because older summaries are in a shape current code cannot read and there is no migration code before 1.0. An upgrade also keeps the summary of a deferred Proposal, which is set aside the same way. Within one store generation a Difference does not change. Retention is below.
7. Produce the comparison explicitly from existing Baseline and Trial evidence using a linear streamed pass and bounded publication transactions. Proposal Finalize does not do this work. One Commands query returns summaries and matrices before indexed, bounded pages; CLI, MCP and the UI program consume it without parsing or reopening live project data.
8. Findings are advisory under ADR 0060. Neither suspicious buckets nor thresholds change Readiness, Apply Authorization or Apply. Missing or conflicting human decisions and incomplete measurements are visible limitations, not clean zero findings.
   A word with no Approved decision keeps `no-approved-expectations` and also carries its observed parse count, so a word that gained or lost every reading still shows a zero. `no-parse` is stated only when there is an Approved expectation to compare against; the parse count alone answers whether a word parsed.

9. Research annotations enter FieldWorks through semantic Intents staged in Proposals and Applied by a person. Reuse existing analysis-create, evaluation and spelling families. Missing Wordform creation or Text/Segment authoring requires a separate complete operation family with closed schema, vectors, coverage, lowering, preview, Apply/read-back, rollback and conflict/diff behavior. An importer never calls LibLCM factories outside that contract or matches entries by gloss/lemma similarity.

10. A Difference is read against one **reference**, recorded on its summary: `human` (the default, FieldWorks' recorded people's decisions) or
    `source-validated` (the decisions a named non-human agent staged from cited sources through Applied Proposals, read from Motif's store as staged
    and never from a person's evaluation or spelling status). The agent is a FieldWorks `CmAgent` with `Human = false`, written by the agent
    evaluation operation; its evidence is a record in the same Proposal. A source-validated Difference is labelled as not a person's in every front
    end (a note on the summary), never merged with or ranked against a human one, and each of its listed words names the evidence tier and agent
    behind the analyses it leans on. That per-word evidence is read from the Applied Proposals when a page is read and is never stored, so the kept
    summary grows only by the reference and a fixed-size fingerprint of the staged decisions it was read against; a new staging of decisions makes
    the next `difference produce` read them again. Where staged agents disagree about an analysis, the word is `conflicting-decisions` whatever the
    order the Proposals were applied in. A Proposal keeps one Difference per Text and reference (store schema 59 widened the uniqueness
    from Proposal and Text), so a Difference against each reference sits beside the other and producing again replaces only the one for the same Text
    and reference. A typo or ill-formed evidence record makes a word form
    incorrect for the source-validated reference only; it is tied to the changed word by its wordform id, which the word detail keeps and which dies
    with it. Only Applied Proposals count, never the compared Proposal's own staged decisions. An Applied revision that cannot be read is named in a
    warning and left out, and the rest still count. The reads are kept per open project and keyed by the Applied revisions, so a page or filter click
    does not parse them again. The reference is chosen by `--reference` on the CLI and by a separate opt-in tool in the `source-validated` MCP profile;
    the default `motif_difference_produce` is unchanged and compares people's decisions.

## Consequences and alternatives

Existing human decisions enable the first useful slice on one Selection without waiting for a broad importer. Frozen comparison data gives later readers the same answer after Opinions change, for as long as the detail is kept.

A live-Opinion read on every page would reinterpret historical results. A spelling-only join would transfer decisions between different Wordforms, while a separate external annotation store contradicts the owner's decision and ADR 0056 ownership.

The first acceptance slice compares one explicit Trial over a small frozen Selection through a shared Commands query, CLI and MCP. Classifier groundwork precedes it; later slices generalize matrices/grouping and supply the data contract to the window program. While the Proposal is undecided, published pages retain the frozen occurrences (the first places of each listed word), human decisions and native case detail of the listed words independently of current indexes or named Selection rows. Missing sample-authoring families remain required work; research's actual sita and deliberately bad mutu acceptance must pass through CLI and MCP before the effort is complete. Thresholds, full spelling capture and domain expansion for Text/Segment authoring remain director decisions.

## First stored slice

The first stored result is made on request and read back from saved tables, with no background job. It compares one completed Trial over one Text.

- **Standalone tables.** The result lives in four tables (`ProposalDifferences`, `ProposalDifferenceCases`, `ProposalDifferenceOccurrences`, `ProposalDifferenceBuckets`) rather than as an Assessment row with a stored scope. Facts are copied by value. The Trial's Assessment ids are provenance, not references: a Difference does not keep those Assessments alive, so applying or deleting a Proposal can remove them (ADR 0042). Deleting a Proposal deletes its Differences. Reports cannot cite a result yet; that arrives only if a Report needs to.
- **Synchronous production.** One command reads the Trial's two ParseTime Assessments and the current Baseline's captured decisions and writes the whole result in one transaction, so nothing partial is ever readable and no claim fencing applies. A Job wrapper replaces this when large-corpus production needs it.
- **One per Proposal, Text and reference.** A new Difference replaces the old one for the same Proposal, Text and reference, and no other. (Before store schema 59 it was one per Proposal and Text.) Producing again for the same Trial, Text, Baseline token and policy returns the saved Difference and writes nothing. A Proposal that has been decided makes no new Difference.
- **Commands.** `difference produce` and `difference show` are AdvancedAi commands, Evaluate and Read, with the MCP tools `motif_difference_produce` and `motif_proposal_difference` in step.
- **Frozen decisions are checked twice.** The command refuses `difference.baseline-changed` when the Baseline that supplied the decisions is not the one the Trial measured, checked on the same read that supplies them.
- **Spelling.** The capture records only whether a wordform is marked incorrect, so every other wordform is read as undecided; Correct is not distinguished from Undecided until the capture says so.
- **Unmatched tokens.** A token binds to a result word only when exactly one of its forms was measured and exactly one Wordform shares that form; every other token is counted as unbound and never guessed. Words the Trial measured that the Text does not contain are counted separately in the summary and are not part of the comparison.

## Scope

A Difference compares one completed Trial's before and after parses, restricted to one named Text, against the Approved expectations captured in the Baseline the Trial measured. Any wider scope is a new Difference shape and a new store generation, not a flag on this one. A Proposal's several Differences are separate results for separate Texts or references; none is a union of the others.

## Window

The window reads the same bounded query as the CLI and MCP and never reclassifies a word. It shows the summary alone once the word detail is gone, and never implies the words exist while they do not.

The Proposal inspector holds the section for every selected Proposal that is not a draft, with or without a saved Difference. A Proposal never tried, or already decided, shows a note instead of the action. A deferred Proposal keeps the action: its words were dropped, the section shows the summary alone and says so, and **Make Difference Again** brings the words back. A person makes a Difference there with an explicit **Make Difference** action for a chosen Text, never automatically after a Trial, because it can be a long job whose word detail is meant to die quickly. The action calls the same `difference produce` command the CLI and MCP call, shows run progress, can be cancelled, and a cancelled run saves nothing. When the Proposal already has a Difference for the chosen Text made against people's decisions, the same action reads **Make Difference Again**. Every Difference shown says which Text it compares and whose decisions it was read against, and when the Proposal has more than one a **Saved Differences** list switches between them; the list is bounded to 50 and counts the rest. A saved Difference whose summary can no longer be read is left out of that list, named in a note by Text, reference and time (`SavedDamaged`), and never stops the others showing: a read without an id shows the newest one that can be read, and making the Difference again for that Text and reference replaces the damaged one. `difference show` and `motif_proposal_difference` list the Proposal's saved Differences with their Text and reference in `Saved`, and take `--text` and `--reference` (`text`, `reference`) to read a particular one; without them they read the newest. The action always compares with a person's decisions and replaces only the saved Difference for that Text and reference, so a Difference an agent made against source-validated decisions is never touched by it. While a Difference is being made the section shows neither the source-validated note nor the intro line, and they return when the run ends. Making a Difference never applies anything. Its counts, matrix, category and limitation chips come from the summary; a chip sends the query's own filter (category, before and after state, limitation, could-be-bad), so the window and the CLI list the same words. A word's Text places are read a page at a time through the same query (`--word`, `motif_proposal_difference` `word`). The lists stop at 100 words and 100 places and ask for a narrower filter, so the section stays small however large a Difference is. With only the summary left its chips count but no longer filter. The window shows approval states and categories in plain labels, never the `delta.*` codes. The older in-session comparison is the Trial summary and keeps its own panel on the Texts page.

## Parser and tokeniser

As ADR 0042's amendment of 2026-10-20 says, a differing parser or tokeniser annotates a comparison and does not stop it. The summary records both sides' parser version and hash, grammar hash, Selection and tokeniser, and carries a warning naming both when the parser hashes differ or the tokeniser differs or either side reran stopped words under a longer limit, because a change in the parser looks exactly like a change in the grammar. A side that recorded no parser is not a mismatch.

## Retention

The owner's size rule: keep a small record of a few kilobytes for each Proposal, as good as forever; with 500 Proposals it must not balloon; the few megabytes of in-depth data must go, and quickly. So a Difference has two parts.

- **Summary.** The matrix, counts, category counts, limitation counts and the pins above. It is kept for as long as the Proposal row exists, whatever the Proposal's state, and a test holds it to 8 KB on a fixture of thousands of words. Nothing that grows with the number of words (word lists, analysis ids) goes in it.
- **Word detail.** The word, occurrence and category rows and each word's facts, stored only for the listed words (below). They are deleted as soon as the Proposal is applied, rejected, withdrawn, superseded or deferred, in the same transaction as the change of state; they are not kept for the last few Proposals. Anything the summary needs is computed before the rows are written. Deferring is the owner's addition of 2026-11-01: a deferred Proposal is set aside, so its words go like a decided one's. Unlike a decided Proposal it can still be compared, so `difference produce` on a deferred Proposal makes the Difference again with its words, and those words last until the Proposal next changes status.

Afterwards `difference show` and `motif_proposal_difference` return the summary with `WordDetailKept` false, an empty word page and no error. `difference produce` for the same Trial, Text, Baseline and policy returns the saved Difference; a different Trial replaces it. A decided Proposal with no matching saved Difference is refused with `difference.proposal-decided`, because the new word detail would be deleted at once. A Proposal that is undecided again can be compared again.

## Stored rows and their bound

The summary counts every compared word; the word rows do not store every word. A word gets rows only when it changed (its before and after states differ, or it has a recorded change), falls in a category, or a search limit touched it. A word that did not change and is in no category is counted and has no row. Each before and after cell, each category and each limitation keeps its best `ProposalDifferenceListing.MostWordsPerGroup` (100) words by rank: the words with the most places in the Text first, the spelling in ordinal order breaking a tie, so the same input stores the same words. A stored word keeps at most `ProposalDifferenceListing.MostPlacesPerWord` (10) of its places, the first in reading order; the word's count of places stays the whole Text's, and the rest are counted but not listed. The stored set is the union over the groups, so a word kept by one group counts as listed in every group it belongs to and a cell can hold more than 100 stored words; a cell whose before and after states are equal can hold rows too, for words in a category. Building a Difference holds a word only while some group keeps it.

The summary and the response say how many words are listed. `WordsListed` on the summary and response, and `Listed` on each cell, category and limitation count, are additive optional fields: a summary saved without them lists every word, so old summaries still read, and the summary stays under 8 KB (about 3 KB measured) with no store schema change. `Listed` on a word's occurrence page is the same kind of field: the page's `Total` is the word's places in the Text, `Listed` is how many are stored, an absent `Listed` means all (a Difference saved before the place bound), and paging ends at the stored places with no empty last page. Every front end says "counted but not listed" with the true numbers wherever a list is cut and never lets a count read as an empty list; a chip for a cell whose before and after states are equal shows its count and does not filter or claim a list, because the words that changed within it are reached through their category chips.

A damaged word row does not hide the summary. `difference show` still returns the summary with `WordDetailKept` false and `WordDetailUnreadable` true, the window says the word detail cannot be read and, while the Proposal can still be compared, offers Make Difference, and `difference produce` makes the Difference again instead of returning the damaged one. When every saved Difference of a Proposal is damaged, `difference show` refuses with `difference.unreadable` and names each by Text, reference and time.

Measured on a synthetic Text, Debug build, with the Difference made in one pass:

| Words compared | Places | Groups filled | Listed words | Place rows | Store added | Summary | Peak working set |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50,002 (uniform) | 50,003 | 6 | 201 | 202 | 1.0 MiB | 2.9 KB | +237 MiB |
| 200,002 (uniform) | 200,003 | 6 | 201 | 202 | 1.0 MiB | 2.9 KB | +150 MiB |
| 688,002 (uniform) | 688,003 | 6 | 201 | 202 | 1.0 MiB | 2.9 KB | +1,697 MiB |
| 200,002 (frequent words) | 381,180 | 20 | 1,201 | 12,001 | 9.0 MiB | 4.7 KB | +607 MiB |
| 688,002 (frequent words) | 1,396,227 | 20 | 1,201 | 12,001 | 9.0 MiB | 4.8 KB | +2,715 MiB |
| 200,002 (frequent words, every limit) | 381,180 | 38 | 2,601 | 23,048 | 15.9 MiB | 6.5 KB | +668 MiB |
| 688,002 (frequent words, every limit) | 1,396,227 | 38 | 2,601 | 25,991 | 16.9 MiB | 6.6 KB | +2,781 MiB |

Before the bound the 688,000-word Text added 2.3 GiB to the store and peaked +2.9 GiB of working set; with 500 words per group and every place stored the frequent-word Text added 183 MiB. A listed word costs about 4 KB in the store (its case, its facts, its category rows and indexes) and a stored place about 0.35 KB. The bound now is the number of groups times 100 words times about 4 KB, plus 10 places each: about 1 MiB when few groups are filled, 9 MiB for 20 groups and 17 MiB for 38 groups, which is nearly every cell, category and limitation there is. The worst case therefore misses the owner's few megabytes by under a factor of two, and the store no longer grows with the Text. The remaining memory is the two sides' Assessments and the Baseline Text, about 7 KB per word, which the pass reads before it classifies a word.

This is why word detail still does not survive a decision: it is the part that grows, the person has decided, and the summary still says what the change did.
