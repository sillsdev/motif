Writing a parser is computer science. You shouldn’t have to do it alone.

Getting a parser to accept a language means turning linguistics into rules a machine follows literally. Motif puts an AI consultant beside you — and gives it everything it needs to be useful: your measured results, the exact reason each word fails, and documentation written for it to read.

You bring
The language
Which words are right, what the morphemes mean, how sounds change. The judgement no machine has.
The AI brings
The machinery
How the parser reads rules, slots and environments; which FieldWorks setting to change; why a grammar is slow; what to try next.
Motif brings
The evidence
Coverage over your texts, the reason every word fails, timing, and a Handoff folder you drag into any AI chat. It checks each fix before it reaches your project.
Works with the AI you already use
Drag the AI Handoff into a chat, or let an agent drive Motif directly: every command answers in JSON, and one call describes them all.
$ motif help --all --json
$ motif handoff Sample.fwdata --out handoff --no-assess
