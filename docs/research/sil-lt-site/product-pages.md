### FieldWorks / FLEx

The landing page leads with the product name and dictionary-creation purpose, then proceeds through “Why Choose FieldWorks?,” three feature sections (“Analyze Texts,” “Describe Grammar,” and “Word Collection”), “Common questions,” and “Recent news posts.” Its opening gives a direct explanation of the work FLEx supports; the feature sections pair short descriptions with images, and the page adds a user testimonial. [FieldWorks home — “FieldWorks Language Explorer™: Dictionary Creation Software,” “Why Choose FieldWorks?,” “Analyze Texts,” “Describe Grammar,” “Word Collection,” “Common questions,” and “Recent news posts”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/)

The “Downloads” page separates “Current Windows” from “Linux-Compatible Releases.” It labels 9.3.10/11 the latest stable release: the standard 64-bit online installer is 9.3.10 and automatically downloads 9.3.11 when a project opens; the offline route uses the 9.3.10 installer and 9.3.11 patch. The page says the offline route suits weak internet or installing on multiple machines. It also says Linux is no longer supported, though FieldWorks 9.0 is available as a Flatpak, and labels the companion FieldWorks Lite as “Beta.” [FieldWorks Downloads — “Current Windows,” “Linux-Compatible Releases,” and “FieldWorks Lite (Beta)”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/); [FieldWorks 9.3.10/11 — “Standard Installation,” “Offline Installation,” and “Help with Installation”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/fw-93/fw-9310/)

Help is a separate “Get Help and Training” destination. It points to web-based documentation, in-app help through F1 or the Help menu, training videos, courses, a user group, and email support; it notes that many videos are older but still useful. [FieldWorks — “Get Help and Training,” “Online Help,” “Training Videos,” “FLEx-List Google Group,” and “Training Courses”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/help/)

## Shared pattern and useful exceptions

**Inferred common template:** every sampled landing page begins with the product name and purpose/tagline, then gives feature information; that is the stable order shared by all six. After features, the page order varies. Five route Downloads and Learn/Help through separate pages, while Speech Analyzer puts downloads, help, support, and contact below its features on one page. No one page has every possible section: news, release notes, requirements, screenshots, videos, forums, and related tools appear in different combinations and locations. The preceding audit gives the observed order and headings for each landing page. **Inferred:** most sampled sites use a product landing page to name the task, show a few feature areas, then separate downloads, learning material, support, and news into navigation. FieldWorks, FLExTrans, Phonology Assistant, and WeSay expose that split directly in their navigation; Speech Analyzer keeps the explanation, download, documentation, and support sections on one page. ## What Motif's site should do

1. **Lead with the task and audience.** Put Motif’s purpose and intended user in the first heading and short paragraph, then show its central workflows. FLEx and Phonology Assistant both open by saying what work the tool supports before describing feature areas. [FieldWorks home — “FieldWorks Language Explorer™: Dictionary Creation Software”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/); [Phonology Assistant home — “Phonology Assistant: Your Go-To Phonetic Data Analysis Software”](https://software.sil.org/phonologyassistant/)
2. **Make the download choice self-explanatory.** If Motif ships more than one build or operating-system option, put the current version, platform, prerequisite, installer kind, and offline option together on its download page. FieldWorks and FLExTrans show how an installer and its FieldWorks prerequisite can be described at the point of choice. [FieldWorks 9.3.10/11 — “Standard Installation,” “Offline Installation,” and “Help with Installation”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/fw-93/fw-9310/); [FLExTrans Downloads — “Current Version”](https://software.sil.org/flextrans/download/)
3. **Give help a permanent, visible route.** Link a short getting-started guide, reference documentation, videos or walkthroughs, FAQ, and support from the product navigation and download page. FieldWorks and Phonology Assistant make those destinations distinct, while WeSay’s Learn page puts quickstart videos together. [FieldWorks — “Get Help and Training”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/help/); [Phonology Assistant Learn — “Getting Started” and “Online Help and Student Guide”](https://software.sil.org/phonologyassistant/learn/); [WeSay Learn — “Videos”](https://software.sil.org/wesay/learn/)
4. **State the release channel and support status plainly.** Identify stable, beta, maintenance-only, or discontinued status next to the version so users can judge whether to install it. SIL’s catalog offers a portfolio-level status vocabulary, and FieldWorks labels its companion app Beta. [Software Products Catalog — “Supported Status”](https://software.sil.org/software-products/); [FieldWorks Downloads — “FieldWorks Lite (Beta)”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/)
5. **Show the workflow with product evidence.** Use a small number of current screenshots or short walkthroughs next to the relevant task descriptions. The sampled pages mix feature images with videos or training clips rather than relying on prose alone. [FieldWorks home — “Analyze Texts,” “Describe Grammar,” and “Word Collection”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/); [FLExTrans home — introductory videos and workflow sections](https://software.sil.org/flextrans/); [WeSay Learn — “Videos” and “Screenshots”](https://software.sil.org/wesay/learn/)
6. **Use one catalog to help people choose, with clear exits for separate products.** A Motif directory could expose task, platform, support state, and related tools before linking to product pages. SIL’s catalog filters by platform, category, and support status, while its Keyman and Paratext entries make the external domains explicit. [Software Products Catalog — “Search & Filter,” “Operating Systems,” “Software Categories,” “Supported Status,” “Keyman,” and “Paratext”](https://software.sil.org/software-products/)
7. **Keep release notes near the current download.** Show what changed and the date beside the offered build; FieldWorks, FLExTrans, and Phonology Assistant link release notes or changes information from the version/download area. [FieldWorks 9.3.10/11 — “Packages For Windows”](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/fw-93/fw-9310/); [FLExTrans Downloads — “Current Version” and “Release Notes”](https://software.sil.org/flextrans/download/); [Speech Analyzer — “Downloads” and “Changes”](https://software.sil.org/speech-analyzer/)
