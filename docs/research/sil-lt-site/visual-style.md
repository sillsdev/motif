# SIL Language Technology site: visual styling

SIL presents its software through a shared Language Technology home and catalog, then gives individual products their own pages for features, downloads, learning, news, and help. **Inferred:** Motif can use that information structure to read as an SIL Language Technology product while making its parser-companion purpose clear. ([Home — “Document & Translate” and “Software & Standards”](https://software.sil.org/); [Software Products Catalog](https://software.sil.org/software-products/); [FieldWorks](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/))

## Scope and evidence

This visual pass uses the source files supplied in `.tmp/sil-raw/`, retrieved **2026-09-28**. The 11 HTML filenames include a zero-byte `home.html` and a `speechanalyzer.html` response with an error-page body; the usable pages cover the catalog, FieldWorks home/download/features, and product pages for WeSay, SayMore, FLExTrans, Phonology Assistant, and Cog. The saved catalog is [software.sil.org/software-products/](https://software.sil.org/software-products/); product pages are [FieldWorks](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/), [WeSay](https://software.sil.org/wesay/), [SayMore](https://software.sil.org/saymore/), [FLExTrans](https://software.sil.org/flextrans/), [Phonology Assistant](https://software.sil.org/phonologyassistant/), and [Cog](https://software.sil.org/cog/). HTML evidence is cited by local file and selector; stylesheet filenames below are under `.tmp/sil-raw/css/`, and `urls.txt` maps each numbered copy to its public URL.

These are stylesheet declarations and source-markup measurements, not browser-computed screenshots. The downloaded files do not include SIL's image assets or a brand manual. The observations below distinguish rules present in CSS from components visibly present in the saved HTML.
