# Research provenance for the Philippine-style sample

This research note records sources considered for a synthetic teaching fixture. It no longer contains selected Tagalog words, paradigms, sentences, or a complete grammar specification.

## Sources and scope

- UniMorph Tagalog data was reviewed at commit `799d79de338d1370ebfbeabb9823a0e8f86a2b6a`; see the [pinned source](https://github.com/UniMorph/tgl/tree/799d79de338d1370ebfbeabb9823a0e8f86a2b6a). The data is CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Research consulted the [Wiktionary Tagalog verb appendix](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Tagalog_verbs) and [affix appendix](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Tagalog_affixes), retrieved 2026-09-26. Wiktionary text is CC BY-SA 4.0 or GFDL.
- Sentence-source research used [Tatoeba downloads](https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads), retrieved 2026-09-26. Tatoeba records use CC BY 2.0 FR and require contributor attribution.

These sources carry separate reuse terms. Any later selection must preserve the relevant attributions and licence obligations.

The former local research tables and their data-specific selection lists were removed. The remaining extraction entrypoints accept a selection manifest supplied outside the repository; Motif stores no such manifest or derived dataset.

This research material is not a product sample and does not establish that any synthetic fixture is authentic or speaker-reviewed Tagalog.
