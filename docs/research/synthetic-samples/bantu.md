# Research provenance for the Bantu-style sample

This research note records sources considered for a synthetic teaching fixture. It no longer contains a sample lexicon, paradigm, sentence, or complete grammar specification.

## Sources and scope

- UniMorph Swahili (`swc`) data was reviewed at commit `02a2bdec0e5cb0dc93b6ef11db4d54a82c34b224`; see the [pinned repository](https://github.com/unimorph/swc/tree/02a2bdec0e5cb0dc93b6ef11db4d54a82c34b224). Its data is CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Sentence-source research used the [Tatoeba downloads](https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads), retrieved 2026-09-26. Tatoeba records use CC BY 2.0 FR and require contributor attribution.
- Linguistic references included [Learning Swahili morphology](https://langsci-press.org/catalog/book/306), the [Swahili Learners' Reference Grammar](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED455681.pdf), [Swahili Grammar and Workbook](https://www.routledge.com/Swahili-Grammar-and-Workbook/Mpiranya/p/book/9780415721069), and [FieldWorks Bantu feature guidance](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/download/bantu_features/).
- Community-maintained word-form research consulted [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights) on 2026-09-26. Its text is CC BY-SA 4.0 or GFDL; the entries are live sources and are not revision-pinned here.
- The cited open scholarly chapter is CC BY 4.0. Other published reference works retain their publishers' terms.

These references are provenance, not an assertion that the synthetic fixture describes Swahili or has speaker approval. The former local source-derived tables and selected examples were removed. No reconstruction manifest is stored in Motif.

## Product fixture

The Bantu-style product sample is supplied by the owner-approved `fixture-inputs-v1` release of `sillsdev/motif-test-grammars`. Motif keeps only its metadata lock at `evals/synthetic-samples.lock.json`; use `./evals/Get-SyntheticSamples.ps1` to provision and verify the external input.
