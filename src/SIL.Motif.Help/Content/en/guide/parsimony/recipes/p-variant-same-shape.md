# Variants of one shape

This check finds five or more added variants that share one variant type, environment, feature and string correspondence. One alternation may cover them. A package adding such a group must carry a recorded rule attempt, or a reviewer holds it.

## Analyse

Group the variants by shape and read the correspondence, such as final-vowel loss, then read whether a rule attempt is recorded for the shape.

## Disposition

An applied keep or defer is tied to this finding's exact evidence. See [Disposition](term:disposition) for how it appears in Active and Suppressed lists and returns after relevant evidence changes.

No disposition mapping exists for this finding yet, so it cannot be recorded with a Notebook disposition. Note the decision in the review instead.

## Ask the linguist

Ask whether a rule was tried, what its prediction test showed, and why it failed.

## Update

No typed action is available for this finding yet. Author the rule and retire the variants it covers, or record the failed rule attempt.

## Verify

Re-run the check and confirm the finding is gone, then confirm that no Approved reading changed.

## Grounding

Describes how an affix attaches to a stem by category; it does not say that a stem without one is wrong, so the finding asks rather than concludes.
