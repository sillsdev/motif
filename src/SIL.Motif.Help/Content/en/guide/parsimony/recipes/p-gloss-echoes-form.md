# Gloss that echoes the form

This check finds senses whose gloss only repeats the entry's form: the form in capitals (`XYZ` on `xyz-`), or the form followed by a dotted suffix (`XYZ.V`). Hyphens, `=` and case are ignored, and any allomorph form of the entry counts. A gloss that adds only a dotted suffix is the mark of a clone entry made to carry a feature.

## Analyse

Read the entry in `allomorph-context` and compare its forms with the sense's glosses in every analysis writing system. Process affixes with no form text are not read, because there is no form to echo.

## Disposition

An applied keep or defer is tied to this finding's exact evidence. See [Disposition](term:disposition) for how it appears in Active and Suppressed lists and returns after relevant evidence changes.

No disposition mapping exists for this finding yet, so it cannot be recorded with a Notebook disposition. Note the decision in the review instead.

## Ask the linguist

Ask what the morpheme means or does, so the gloss can say it. Include the entry, its form and the gloss that echoes it. A dotted suffix suggests a clone entry: ask whether it should be merged with its homograph.

## Update

No typed action is available for this finding. Replace the gloss with a meaningful one in FieldWorks, or merge the clone into its homograph.

## Verify

Re-run the check and confirm the sense is no longer listed.
