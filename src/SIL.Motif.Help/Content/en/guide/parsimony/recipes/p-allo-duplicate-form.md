# Repeated allomorph forms

This check finds non-abstract allomorphs of one entry with the same morph type, the same forms in every writing system, and the same environment list. Different environment lists are excluded from this finding.

## Analyse

Read the entry, duplicate allomorph GUIDs, forms, morph type, and exact environment lists in `allomorph-context`. Confirm the matching fields; no similarity or language-wide generalization is inferred.

## Disposition

An applied keep or defer is tied to this finding's exact evidence. See [Disposition](term:disposition) for how it appears in Active and Suppressed lists and returns after relevant evidence changes.

Classify the result as exact data duplication. Keep both records when each carries identity or history worth preserving; ask or defer when that distinction is unclear.

## Ask the linguist

Ask whether both records are intentionally maintained despite having identical forms and conditions. Include the entry and every duplicate GUID.

## Update

Retire a duplicate only through an operation that preserves and retargets its Approved analyses. If that operation is unavailable, keep the records and defer the change.

## Verify

Confirm the exact duplicate group is gone and every retargeted Approved reading remains unchanged. Compare before and after evidence for the same Baseline scope.

## Grounding

Engineering check: exact redundancy in the authored grammar; no linguistic claim.

Describes allomorph environments and ordered alternatives, without establishing that any duplicate is linguistically redundant.
