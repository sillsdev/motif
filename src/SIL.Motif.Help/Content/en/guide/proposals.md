# Proposals

**Proposals** lists every set of changes you can review: the Proposals an AI assistant put together, and the changes you collected yourself that are not applied yet. Each card shows what it changes and four numbers, today's beside what they would be with the Proposal.

The cards are grouped by what to do next:

- **Waiting for you** has been tried against the project as it is now and is ready to read.
- **Needs another try** was tried, but the project or the Proposal changed since, or the try did not finish. Its numbers are from that try, and nothing re-runs until you ask.
- **Not tried yet** has no Trial. Choose **Try it** to measure it.
- **Your changes not applied yet** are the changes you collected on **Texts**; they are also listed on **Review changes**.
- **Closed** lists the Proposals that were applied, declined, superseded or withdrawn. They are hidden until you choose **Show closed**, and **Hide closed** hides them again.

A card names what the Proposal changes in one line, such as “2 rules · 1 allomorph · 1 parse approved”. Its four numbers are Text Coverage, approved words kept, word time and warnings. A number that moved is shown in bold. A chip can say that approved words were lost, that a change no longer fits, or that another Proposal also changes some of the same words. A Proposal that only approves or removes analyses measures only the words it touches; every other word keeps today's value, so its totals still compare with today's.

Choose **Open** to see the project's pages with the Proposal beside today: the numbers on **Overview**, **Texts**, **Timing** and **Warnings** show today's value and the Proposal's. Choose **Try again** on a card that needs another try to measure it on a copy of the project. The copy is thrown away; your FieldWorks project is not changed.

Each card beside its name says where the Proposal is: **Draft**, **Ready**, **Deferred**, **Applied**, **Declined**, **Superseded** or **Withdrawn**. Your own changes carry no such word. A declined or withdrawn card offers **Reopen**, which makes it a Draft again with its history kept. A declined, superseded or withdrawn card offers **Delete**, which removes the Proposal and its tries. An applied Proposal cannot be deleted; it and its record of what was applied are kept.

The number beside **Proposals** in the sidebar counts the cards that are waiting for you.
