# Proposals

**Proposals** lists every set of changes you can review: the Proposals an AI assistant put together, and the changes you collected yourself that are not applied yet. Its table shows who built each one, its status and, when it has a Trial, four measures from before and after.

Use the filters to see three groups:

- **Your changes not applied yet** are the changes you collected on **Texts**; they are also listed on **Review Changes**.
- **Waiting for you** includes Proposals with a current Trial, Proposals that need another try, and Proposals that have not been tried. Choose **Try it** or **Try again** to measure one on a copy of the project; nothing re-runs until you ask.
- **Closed** lists Proposals that were applied, declined, superseded or withdrawn. Select **Closed** to see those Proposals. When closed Proposals exist, **Show closed** includes them with the other groups and clears the current filter; **Hide closed** hides them and clears the filter.

At wide widths, each row shows the Proposal's name and number of changes. At narrow widths, the table keeps the name, status and actions; choose **Details** from the row's **⋯** menu to read its counts and other facts in the Inspector. Its measures are approved words kept, Text Coverage, warnings and **Work**, the parser work in parser work, with today's value beside the value from the last try. **Work** is blank when the Trial cannot be compared with today's parse, for example when a search was stopped; a blank number says why in its tooltip. The Inspector adds **Time on this computer**, which changes with how busy the computer is and so is only a second figure. A number that moved is shown in bold. When there is no Trial, the table says **Not tried yet**. A chip can say that approved words were lost, that a change no longer fits, or that another Proposal also changes some of the same words. A Proposal that only approves or removes analyses measures only the words it touches; every other word keeps today's value, so its totals still compare with today's.

Select a row to choose the Proposal's details. When the Inspector is collapsed, choose **Details** from the row's **⋯** menu to reveal them. The Inspector shows its status, who built it, a summary of its changes and the numbers from its last try. Choose **Open in Review Changes** there to review the Proposal. Choose **Open** on a row to open the Proposal: a bar across the window names it and has **Leave Proposal**, which returns every page to today; **Esc** does the same when nothing else is using it, so in a text box, with a menu open or inside a dialog it does that job first and the Proposal stays open. Once the Proposal has been tried, the bar also says when, and on which saved state of the FieldWorks project, such as "Tried Fri 9 Oct, 08:00 on Baseline of Tue 22 Sep, 06:00", and then whether that try is still current. A Proposal that was never tried has no such sentence. The bar's **Today**, **Today → Proposal** and **Proposal** buttons choose what **Overview** shows; they appear only while **Overview**, **Texts**, **Warnings** or **Timing** is the page you are on, and the choice is kept while you move between pages until you leave the Proposal. On **Overview**, the four headline numbers (Text Coverage, Approved Analyses Kept, Speed and Grammar Warnings) show, by that choice, today's value, today's value and then the Proposal's, such as 7 → 8 of 9 words parse, or the Proposal's value alone. Under **Proposal** each headline stands alone, and the rows and captions that describe today's parse give way to a line saying that today's breakdown is in the **Today** view; the rest of the page is today's, and the note under the page title says which of these you are reading. A headline the Proposal has no value for says so, and never shows today's number in its place; under **Today → Proposal** it shows today's alone, as the note says. When today has no parse but the Proposal has one, **Today → Proposal** and **Proposal** show the tiles, with today's side reading as none, and **Today** keeps the page that asks you to parse; when neither has a parse the note says so. When the Proposal has not been tried, or its try did not finish, **Overview** says so. **Warnings** pairs today's grammar findings with the Proposal's own check: each count reads today's and then the Proposal's, findings only today has are struck through, marked **Gone** and listed under **Gone with This Proposal**, and findings only the Proposal has are marked **New** and listed under **New with This Proposal**. The marks are words in the row, and the row's **Details** and **⋯** buttons carry them in their names, so they do not depend on colour or the strikethrough. Findings are matched by their kind, so one finding of a kind today and a different finding of the same kind in the Proposal are neither gone nor new; the counts can then read the same on both sides. **Touch your words** counts the words of the rows shown in that view, and changing the view keeps the selected finding, the sort and the filters. **Timing** pairs the total word time, the median and the number of words stopped at the work limit, and the work by kind, in parser work, but only when both sides were read for the same words, as the note says. When the two sides cover different words, for example the slowest words or the words that stopped, nothing is paired and the page shows today's numbers and says so. When two tries cannot be compared in parser work, **Timing** pairs only the words stopped at the work limit and keeps today's totals; under **Proposal** the total word time, the median and the work by kind then read none, and the stopped words stay the Proposal's. The bar, the rule table and its times stay today's, as the note says, and a kind only one side has reads as none on the other side. Each page reads the Proposal's side only while it is shown, once for the Proposal, its try and the words chosen, and says it is reading until that read lands; it never shows an earlier Proposal's number in its place. On both pages a number the Proposal lacks says so and never shows today's in its place. On **Texts** the same choice applies to the matrix on the Matrix tab: under **Today → Proposal** each cell, each row total and each column total reads today's count and then the Proposal's, such as 12 → 9, and a cell whose count moved is outlined; under **Proposal** the cells and totals show the Proposal's counts alone. The places under a cell follow the same choice: today's places then the Proposal's, such as 5 → 3 places, or the Proposal's alone, taken from the words the Proposal puts in that cell; where its try measured words that today's matrix has no places for, the cell shows a dash rather than a number that would read as complete. The words listed under the matrix follow the same choice. Under **Today** they are today's. Under **Today → Proposal** each word gains a **With This Proposal** column that says where the Proposal put it, such as **Same** or **No parse**, or **Not measured with this Proposal** when its try did not measure that word; when the Proposal measured the same words as today's matrix, a word is listed if either side puts it in a chosen cell, so a cell that reads 0 → 5 lists those five words, and when it measured different words the list stays today's. Under **Proposal** the list shows the Proposal's own words in the cells you chose, with the **With This Proposal** column and the Proposal's time in parser work, and sorting by the slowest orders by those steps; the chips, the fix-first list and the buttons that parse or propose again give way, because they act on today's words, the shared morphemes give way whenever the list holds the Proposal's words, and the list says nothing until the Proposal's counts have been read. A word only the Proposal's try measured, which today's matrix does not have, is counted in its cell but cannot be listed, so the heading after the list's words says how many are counted but not listed, and the other numbers stay true. Counts are paired only when the Proposal's try measured the same words as today's matrix, as the note says; when it measured different words nothing is paired and the cells show today's counts. A pair compares counts, so a cell that reads the same on both sides can still hold different words, and a cell that reads 0 → 5 is not drawn faint. While the Proposal's counts are being read the note says so and the cells show today's counts. If the keyboard focus was in the chips or the fix-first list when you switched to **Proposal**, it moves to a cell of the matrix. A Proposal with no try shows its cells as none, never as zero. The Proposal's counts are read once for the Proposal and its try, only while **Texts** is shown, and the page says it is reading until they land; if they cannot be read the note says so and they are read again the next time you show **Texts** or the evidence is published. Changing the view keeps the chosen cells, the sort and the filters. On **Lists** the same choice applies to each list: under **Today → Proposal** a list's chip and header read today's words and then the Proposal's, such as 5 → 3 words · 8 → 6 places, and under **Proposal** they read the Proposal's alone, and a list with no words in the number drawn drops its chip unless it is the list you are reading. Where the Proposal's try measured words that today's matrix has no places for, the header says the places are not counted with this Proposal and never reads them as zero. The chosen list shows the words the choice lists for its cells, a line under its header says which, the **With This Proposal** column says where the Proposal puts each word, and **AI Handoff for This List** sends the words shown. A chip's name says which number is today's and which is with this Proposal. Under **Today → Proposal** and **Proposal**, the **Moved By This Proposal** chip beside the sort on **Matrix** and **Lists** narrows the words to those the Proposal puts in a different cell than today does, over the words both sides have; its number counts them in the chosen cells, it hides when there are none unless it is chosen, a word only one side has is never counted, and under **Today** every word is listed again while the choice waits for you to return. **AI Handoff** sends the words listed, and leaving or switching the Proposal drops the choice. **What Changed** under **Today → Proposal** lists the words that went from one cell today to another with the Proposal: each move reads today's cell and then the Proposal's, with how many words moved, and choosing a move lists its words, each with today's parse and the cell the Proposal puts it in. Only words that today's matrix and the Proposal's try both have are compared; the words only one side has are counted under the summary and never listed as moved. Under **Today** the tab keeps comparing the last two runs; under **Today → Proposal** and under **Proposal** it lists the same moves, since a move is a word's cell today and its cell with the Proposal. The moves are found once for the Proposal and today's words, only while **What Changed** is shown beside the Proposal, and are let go when you leave the Proposal; a flip between the two views reads nothing. The note is not shown when there is no matrix; the word cards show the Proposal's block as before. Every other page shows today's numbers while a Proposal is open. If the bar is too narrow for one row it wraps to two, so **Try Again** and **Leave Proposal** stay in the window. The copy used for a Trial is thrown away; your FieldWorks project is not changed.

Below those numbers the Inspector shows a **Difference** section for every Proposal you select that is not a draft. A Difference compares what the Proposal's last try built with the Approved analyses saved in FieldWorks, for one Text. It is advice only: it never changes the project and never applies anything. Choose a Text, then choose **Make Difference**. It is never made for you after a try, because it can take a while. A bar shows how many words are compared, and **Cancel** stops it; a cancelled run saves nothing. When it is ready the section shows how many words to check and how many improved, with chips that list those words and, for one word, where it occurs in the Text. A Proposal keeps one Difference for each Text and each set of decisions it was compared with, so several sit side by side: making one for another Text replaces nothing. When there is more than one, a **Saved Differences** list switches between them, and every Difference says which Text it compares and whether it was read against the Approved analyses people saved in FieldWorks or against a program's decisions. **Make Difference** always compares with people's decisions, and the same button reads **Make Difference Again** when the Proposal already has one for the chosen Text; it replaces only that one. A Proposal that was never tried shows a note asking you to try it first, with no button. Once a Proposal is decided, only the Difference's summary is kept, the section says so, and a new one cannot be made. A deferred Proposal also keeps only the summary, and its words are not shown; the section says so and **Make Difference Again** brings the words back until the Proposal's status next changes.

Below the buttons the Inspector shows a **Parsimony** section for every Proposal you select that is not a draft. Parsimony is advice about the grammar, and it never changes the project, **Readiness** or **Apply**. A Trial that finishes queues it by itself; nothing in this window starts it or a parse. The section says where the advice stands: **Not Set Up**, **Not Yet Run**, **Queued**, **Running** (with its step when the job reports one), **Ready**, **Out of Date** (with what changed) or **Did Not Finish** (with why). It updates while the job runs, and keeps checking more slowly if the job takes a long time, until the state is final or you select another Proposal; a busy project does not stop it. Advice that did not finish is only advice that did not finish: the Trial, **Readiness** and **Apply** are not affected. The **Grammar Group** shows its own state and, under **Counts by Measure**, how many findings each measure found new, resolved, unchanged or with changed evidence; counts are per measure and are not added together. The **Text Group** does not run on its own, so it says it is not set up or not yet run. **Ready** advice does not check a changed human judgment or Parsimony Selection, so it can be behind on those. The section shows no start time or duration.

The status column says **Draft** for your changes and **Ready**, **Deferred**, **Applied**, **Declined**, **Superseded** or **Withdrawn** for other rows. A declined or withdrawn Proposal offers **Reopen**, which makes it a Draft again with its history kept. A declined, superseded or withdrawn Proposal offers **Delete**, which removes it and its tries. An applied Proposal cannot be deleted; it and its record of what was applied are kept. The **⋯** menu repeats each row's available actions.

The number beside **Proposals** in the sidebar counts Proposals whose Trial is current. While a Proposal is open, **Texts**, **Warnings** and **Timing** also show a purple badge: Texts counts the net change in the words in Same (the Proposal's Same words less today's), Warnings the findings the Proposal gains or loses, and Timing the percentage change in parser work over the same words, each as "+" or "−" from today's. A page shows its badge once it has read the Proposal's side, and none when nothing changed or the two sides cover different words. Its tooltip and a screen reader say what the badge counts and that it is the Proposal's number.
