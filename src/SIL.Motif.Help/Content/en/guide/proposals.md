# Proposals

**Proposals** lists every set of changes you can review: the Proposals an AI assistant put together, and the changes you collected yourself that are not applied yet. Its table shows who built each one, its status and, when it has a Trial, four measures from before and after.

Use the filters to see three groups:

- **Your changes not applied yet** are the changes you collected on **Texts**; they are also listed on **Review changes**.
- **Waiting for you** includes Proposals with a current Trial, Proposals that need another try, and Proposals that have not been tried. Choose **Try it** or **Try again** to measure one on a copy of the project; nothing re-runs until you ask.
- **Closed** lists Proposals that were applied, declined, superseded or withdrawn. Select **Closed** to see those Proposals. When closed Proposals exist, **Show closed** includes them with the other groups and clears the current filter; **Hide closed** hides them and clears the filter.

At wide widths, each row shows the Proposal's name and number of changes. At narrow widths, the table keeps the name, status and actions; choose **Details** from the row's **⋯** menu to read its counts and other facts in the Inspector. Its measures are approved words kept, Text Coverage, warnings and word time, with today's value beside the value from the last try. A number that moved is shown in bold. When there is no Trial, the table says **Not tried yet**. A chip can say that approved words were lost, that a change no longer fits, or that another Proposal also changes some of the same words. A Proposal that only approves or removes analyses measures only the words it touches; every other word keeps today's value, so its totals still compare with today's.

Select a row to choose the Proposal's details. When the Inspector is collapsed, choose **Details** from the row's **⋯** menu to reveal them. The Inspector shows its status, who built it, a summary of its changes and the numbers from its last try. Choose **Open in Review changes** there to review the Proposal. Choose **Open** on a row to see the project's pages with the Proposal beside today: the numbers on **Overview**, **Texts**, **Timing** and **Warnings** show today's value and the Proposal's. The copy used for a Trial is thrown away; your FieldWorks project is not changed.

The status column says **Draft** for your changes and **Ready**, **Deferred**, **Applied**, **Declined**, **Superseded** or **Withdrawn** for other rows. A declined or withdrawn Proposal offers **Reopen**, which makes it a Draft again with its history kept. A declined, superseded or withdrawn Proposal offers **Delete**, which removes it and its tries. An applied Proposal cannot be deleted; it and its record of what was applied are kept. The **⋯** menu repeats each row's available actions.

The number beside **Proposals** in the sidebar counts Proposals whose Trial is current.
