# Review a Proposal

Select a Proposal in **Proposals**. Read its Rationale and supported effects above **Comments**. You can keep several documents open, including Drafts and Waiting Proposals, without sharing their edits or Trial results.

The evidence names its Text and any limits. **Will Fill In** requires complete searches on both sides. **Will Change** requires an actual reading or spelling acceptance change. The window does not infer approved places lost from a regression count. Missing detail is unavailable, and incomplete listings do not establish a total of zero.

Comments use FieldWorks Markdown. While a Proposal is open, people may add, edit or delete Comments. A failed save keeps your typing. Refresh reconciles external changes without overwriting an edit; if the Comment changed elsewhere, cancel to read its stored version. Once the Proposal is decided, Comments freeze. Any unsaved typing stays available to copy or cancel.

Use **Before you apply** to follow the three checks on the Proposal document. Choose **Apply…** to open the confirmation once the checks and readiness rules are met. Only confirming **Apply** writes to FieldWorks. See [Apply to FieldWorks](guide:apply-to-fieldworks).
