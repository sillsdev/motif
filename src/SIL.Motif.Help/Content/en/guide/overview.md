# Overview

**Overview** is the first page when you open a project. It gathers stored results and project counts so you can see where things stand without starting a new run.

Use **Look first** to open the saved words or timings behind a measured group. A word can hold Unknown and Disapproved analyses at the same time; its Matrix row summarizes what FieldWorks holds, while each analysis keeps its own opinion. If a completed search builds only the Disapproved analysis, the word belongs to **Unknown × Different**, with **Rebuilt an analysis you Disapproved** and **Your undecided analysis wasn't built**. Compare the analyses before deciding what opinion to record. A stopped search does not establish which analyses were not built.

The top card shows the project and its counts: words in the Selection, occurrences, wordforms, rules, and lexemes. Four cards follow. **Speed** shows how many words PanGloss parsed, their total word time (each word's parse time added up), the median time for a word, and the slowest words. **Text coverage** shows how many of the words in your Selection parse, and how many of their occurrences. **Approved analyses kept** shows how many of the words you approved in FieldWorks the grammar still builds. **Grammar warnings** shows the findings from a grammar check and the errors among them. These are separate measures, not one overall score.

The top bar says whether these numbers are current. If FieldWorks has saved since they were measured, the Overview says so too. Press **Refresh** when you are ready to capture the saved project and measure the [Default Selection](term:default-selection) again. If Motif cannot read the numbers, the page says so in one line, with **Try again** and **Report a problem**.

Choose a card's link, such as **Timing →**, **Texts →**, **Matrix →**, or **Warnings →**, to see more on that page. Expand **Project history** to see recorded activity.
