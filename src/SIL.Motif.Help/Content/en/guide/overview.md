# Overview

**Overview** is the first page when you open a project. It gathers stored results and project counts so you can see where things stand without starting a new run.

The top card shows the project and its counts: words in the Selection, occurrences, wordforms, rules, and lexemes. Four cards follow. **Speed** shows how many words PanGloss parsed and how long they took, the median time for a word, and the slowest words. **Text coverage** shows how many of the words in your Selection parse, and how many of their occurrences. **Approved analyses kept** shows how many of the words you approved in FieldWorks the grammar still builds. **Grammar warnings** shows the findings from a grammar check and the errors among them. These are separate measures, not one overall score.

The top bar says whether these numbers are current. If FieldWorks has saved since they were measured, the Overview says so too. Press **Refresh** when you are ready to capture the saved project and measure the [Default Selection](term:default-selection) again. If Motif cannot read the numbers, the page says so in one line, with **Try again** and **Report a problem**.

Choose a card's link, such as **Timing →**, **Texts →**, **Matrix →**, or **Warnings →**, to see more on that page. Expand **Project history** to see recorded activity.
