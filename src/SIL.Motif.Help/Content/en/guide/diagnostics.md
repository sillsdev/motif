# Diagnostics

Turn on Diagnostics in the health details or the View menu to add this page to the rail. Inspect the stored grammar check and recorded parser work here. The choice belongs to you and starts off. Turning Diagnostics off closes Diagnostics and removes diagnostic details from the window.

Diagnostics has exactly two tabs: **Warnings** and **Speed by Rule**. Opening the page does not start a parse or a grammar check. Opening either tab reads stored evidence and never starts Parsimony. Missing or outdated evidence is unavailable, rather than a count of zero.

In **Warnings**, select a recorded kind to read its explanation, named FieldWorks objects and attributed words. **Show Words** brings the selected warning's recorded word evidence into view. Exact uses, possible membership matches and spelling matches remain distinct. **Hide This Warning** hides a finding for you only; unhide it under **Settings → Hidden Warnings**. The warning count shows how many are hidden, and hiding never changes the grammar.

**Speed by Rule** shows recorded parser steps per measured word. Its denominator says how many selected words recorded work. The unattributed remainder stays visible. A missing denominator leaves the count unavailable. Partial per-word detail is labelled even when complete stored aggregate totals can be shown. Select a rule to resolve its exact **Open in FieldWorks** destination; unresolved objects have no link.

Rules are read and edited in FieldWorks. Warnings and Parsimony are advice and add no condition to Apply. A Proposal's Diagnostics uses only evidence bound to that Proposal's content and Trial. Missing, inconclusive or outdated Parsimony is never described as no change. Signed grammar-size changes remain unavailable when the stored contract does not supply them.
