# Diagnostics

Turn on Diagnostics in the health details or the **View** menu to add this page to the rail. **Warnings** lists findings from the stored grammar check. **Speed by Rule** shows available recorded parser work for rules. Opening the page does not start a parse or a grammar check.

Turning Diagnostics off hides its page and returns to Progress when Diagnostics was selected. Missing or stale measurements are left out rather than presented as zero.
