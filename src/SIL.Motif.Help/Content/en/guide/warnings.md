# Warnings

**Warnings** shows findings from a check of the whole grammar. They are stored for the [Baseline](term:baseline), and do not depend on which texts or words you selected. Each finding describes what PanGloss noticed; error-level findings can identify grammar problems that prevent parsing.

If the grammar has not been checked for this Baseline, choose **Check the grammar**. The parser reads the whole grammar, so a check can take a minute for a large project. **Refresh** also checks the grammar after capturing a Baseline. Later visits use the stored findings until the Baseline changes; opening the page does not start a check by itself.

Use **Where...** to narrow findings by where they apply, or **What it means...** to search by the problem described. Select a kind of finding to read **What it means**. Choose a finding to open it on **What to do in FieldWorks**, PanGloss's advice for it, and **After you fix it**: save in FieldWorks, press **Refresh**, and the finding is gone once the fix works. When PanGloss names no single item, the finding says so and names the place in FieldWorks to look. Error-level findings are shown separately from warnings and information. A warning describes what the parser reported; use your linguistic judgment to decide what to investigate or change.

![Filtered grammar findings](shot:warnings-filter/filtered-findings)

PanGloss supplies each finding's title, explanation and advice in the report's recorded language. **Learn more** shows its background when available, and **PanGloss reference** opens the documentation for the supported parser release. FieldWorks places come from the report's verified destinations, independently of how the advice is worded.

A missing reference is shown as unavailable and cannot open FieldWorks. A project setting stays a setting, with no individual object link. A named live object opens only through the destination PanGloss supplied. None of these states is inferred from the warning's sentence.
