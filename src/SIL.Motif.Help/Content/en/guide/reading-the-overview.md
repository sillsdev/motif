# Reading the Overview

The [Overview](term:overview) brings together saved results and project counts. **Text coverage** tells you how many of your chosen words parse and how often they occur in your selected texts. **Approved analyses kept** tells you how many words you approved in FieldWorks the grammar still builds. A high Text coverage number with a lower Approved analyses kept number means many chosen words parse, but fewer approved analyses were found; open **Texts** and inspect the words because the cards measure different things. A slow word is a place to investigate, not an answer about what to change. Broad slots or environments may be worth checking, but timing does not identify a fix. **Grammar warnings** lists what the grammar check found; read each finding before deciding whether to change the grammar.

For the full page tour—including freshness, timing, warnings and the links behind each card—see [Overview](guide:overview). Opening the page shows stored results; it does not start a parse. After saving in FieldWorks, press **Refresh**, then **Parse all words** to measure your current [Default Selection](term:default-selection) against the saved project. See [Parse all words](guide:assessment).
