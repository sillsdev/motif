# Reading the Overview

The **Overview** brings together the results Motif has already stored. The top of the page shows the project, the number of words in the Selection, text occurrences, and counts of wordforms, rules, and lexemes. Its cards are **Speed**, **Text coverage**, **Approved analyses kept**, and **Grammar warnings**.

Speed is PanGloss's own measured time: how many words it parsed, their total word time (each word's parse time added up, not how long the run took), the median time for a word, how many stopped at the step limit, and the slowest words. Text coverage reports how many selected words and text occurrences parsed; a word that stopped hit a step or a time limit. Approved analyses kept says how many of the words you approved in FieldWorks the grammar still builds, how many it lost, any disapproved analysis it still builds, and how many words marked Unknown PanGloss confirms. Grammar warnings are findings from a grammar check, the same number the Warnings page shows beside its name; they are about the grammar, not a count of errors in the selected texts. These numbers answer different questions, so read the labels and breakdowns rather than comparing the headline values as if they were one score.

The top bar says whether the stored results are current. If FieldWorks has saved changes since they were measured, the freshness line gives the save time and the Baseline time once; press **Refresh** there to capture the saved project again and measure the current [Default Selection](term:default-selection). Refresh is an explicit action; simply reopening the window does not rerun the parser. If Motif cannot read the numbers, the page says so in one line and offers **Try again** and **Report a problem**.

Open **Project history** near the bottom of the page to see recorded activity. Choose a card's link, such as **Texts →**, **Timing →**, or **Warnings →**, to inspect that information on its page.
