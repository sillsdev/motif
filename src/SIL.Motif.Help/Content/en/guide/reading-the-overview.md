# Reading the Overview

The [Overview](term:overview) brings together saved results and project counts. Its cards answer different questions: how long words took to parse, how many words in your Selection parse, how many Approved analyses the grammar still builds, and what the grammar check found. Read each card's labels and breakdowns separately; the headline numbers are not one combined score.

For the full page tour—including freshness, Text Coverage, timing, warnings and the links behind each card—see [Overview](guide:overview). Opening the page shows stored results; it does not start a parse. To measure the current [Default Selection](term:default-selection) against the saved FieldWorks project, choose **Refresh** or **Parse all words** as described in [Parse all words](guide:assessment).
