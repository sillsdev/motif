# Reading the Overview

The [Overview](term:overview) brings together saved results and project counts. **Text coverage** tells you how many of your chosen words parse and how often they occur in your selected texts. **Approved analyses kept** counts words with an Approved analysis that the grammar rebuilt exactly. Its detail line counts words whose forms and glosses match but whose recorded entries differ when entry evidence is available, as well as **Lost**, **Stopped**, and **Not parsed** outcomes when those results are recorded. A high Text coverage number with a lower Approved analyses kept number means many chosen words parse, but fewer approved analyses were rebuilt exactly; open **Texts** and inspect the words because the cards measure different things. A slow word is a place to investigate, not an answer about what to change. Broad slots or environments may be worth checking, but timing does not identify a fix. **Grammar warnings** lists what the grammar check found; read each finding before deciding whether to change the grammar.

Times below ten seconds use milliseconds; longer times use seconds to one decimal. **Look first** opens the saved words or timings behind a measured group. It calls out Approved words whose forms and glosses match but whose recorded entries differ when entry evidence is available, as well as words Lost and searches that stopped. In a word row, Motif marks the differing PanGloss morpheme; when both entry names are recorded, its tooltip names them. When a word has Unknown and Disapproved analyses, its Matrix row summarizes FieldWorks status while each analysis keeps its approval status. In **Unknown × Different**, the cell says **Have a look** when PanGloss matched an analysis marked Disapproved and shows the recorded comparison detail. A stopped search does not establish which analyses were not built.

For the full page tour—including freshness, timing, warnings and the links behind each card—see [Overview](guide:overview). Opening the page shows stored results; it does not start a parse. After saving in FieldWorks, press **Refresh**, then **Parse all words** to measure your current [Default Selection](term:default-selection) against the saved project. See [Parse all words](guide:assessment).
