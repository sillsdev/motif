# Reading the Overview

The **Overview** brings together the results Motif has already stored. The top of the page shows the project, the number of words in the Selection, places in the chosen Texts, and counts of wordforms, rules, and lexemes. Its cards are **Speed**, **Text coverage**, **Approved analyses kept**, and **Grammar warnings**.

Speed is PanGloss's measured word time: how many words it parsed, their total word time (each word's parse time added up, not how long the run took), the median time for a word, how many stopped at the step limit, and a compact line of the slowest words and their recorded times. A word stopped at the step limit is marked **Stopped** in that line. Text coverage reports how many selected words and places parsed; a word that stopped hit a step or a time limit. Approved analyses kept says how many of the words you approved in FieldWorks the grammar still builds, how many it lost, any disapproved analysis it still builds, and how many words marked Unknown PanGloss confirms. Grammar warnings reports the warning, error, and information finding counts from the stored grammar check. They are findings about the grammar, not a count of errors in the selected Texts; their total matches the number beside Warnings in the sidebar. The line about your words counts the distinct words matched by exact identity to named items, the same count shown by **Touch your words** on the Warnings page when the evidence is available. The page keeps words that use members of a named resource and words matched by spelling alone separate from confirmed uses; spelling does not confirm that the item was used, and incomplete evidence is identified. These numbers answer different questions, so read the labels and breakdowns rather than comparing the headline values as if they were one score.

The top bar says whether the stored results are current. If FieldWorks has saved changes since they were measured, the freshness line gives the save time and the Baseline time once; press **Refresh** there to capture the saved project again and measure the current [Default Selection](term:default-selection). Refresh is an explicit action; simply reopening the window does not rerun the parser. If Motif cannot read the numbers, the page says so in one line and offers **Try again** and **Report a problem**.

Open **Project history** near the bottom of the page to see recorded activity. Choose a card's link, such as **Texts →**, **Slowest words →**, or **Warnings →**, to inspect that information on its page.
