# Reading project health

The health strip reports facts available from the saved project and stored results. A warning is a finding to inspect, not an instruction to change the grammar. Open **Diagnostics** when enabled to read stored warnings and rule timing.

Parser work measures the work recorded by the parser. It is distinct from elapsed time on a particular computer. A missing or stale measurement does not mean that the parser did no work; Motif leaves unavailable health facts out. The median, when recorded, is in the health details.

Opening these views does not run the parser. After saving in FieldWorks, choose **Refresh** to read the saved project, then parse the current Selection when you need new results. See [Progress and project health](guide:overview).

The Progress page’s Next rows open waiting Proposals and open Requests. Their counts come from stored records, and rows disappear while those records are read again. Neither parser completion nor a wordform’s approved analysis proves that a person approved every occurrence. See [Progress](guide:progress).
