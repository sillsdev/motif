# Text Coverage

[Text Coverage](term:text-coverage) shows what share of the Default Selection’s words, and their places in the chosen Texts, parsed in the last saved parse. The Overview reports both selected words and their places, so the two numbers may differ: one word form can appear many times in a Text.

Use the breakdown to see parsed words alongside words with no parse, incomplete searches, or other recorded outcomes. Digit-only places and places in other writing systems are counted apart in a line beneath the share, from the Text summaries saved with the Baseline. A word the parser refuses as an invalid shape stays in the share. A word that stopped at the work limit or the time safety net did not complete its search, so read that outcome separately from a completed search with no parse.

**Text Coverage by Frequency Band** splits the same share by how often each word occurs, so you can see whether the words you use most parse. Words that occur equally often are ranked by spelling, so the bands are the same every time. A Selection with fewer words than a band has only the bands it fills. A Selection saved from a word-list file with counts is ranked by those counts instead, is titled **By Frequency Band**, and is never called Text Coverage, because the counts are the file's and not places in a Text. Text Coverage is about the selected Texts and words. It is not the same as **Accuracy**, which compares parser readings with approved analyses, or grammar coverage over an outside Corpus. Choose **Texts** to inspect words and places; read [Parse all words](term:parse-all-words) to understand which run the Overview is showing.
