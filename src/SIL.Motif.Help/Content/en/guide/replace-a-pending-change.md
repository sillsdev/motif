# Replace a Pending Change

Return to the word in **Texts** and undo the staged choice you want to replace. Read its current context before making another choice. **Undo** affects staging only and does not reverse an applied Proposal.

After replacing a staged change, measure the exact new content again before applying. Earlier evidence does not establish readiness for the replacement. Read [Pending Changes](guide:pending-changes) and [Apply to FieldWorks](guide:apply-to-fieldworks).
