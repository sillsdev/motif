# PanGloss

On Analyze texts, PanGloss runs the project's grammar on selected texts and compares its analyses with what FieldWorks stores.

PanGloss parses XAmple and HermitCrab grammars fast. Fully compatible.

A means Approved, U means Unknown, D means Disapproved, and a dashed mark means FieldWorks has no analysis stored. Blue means PanGloss found a new or different analysis. Staged means a choice is waiting for Apply to FieldWorks project.
