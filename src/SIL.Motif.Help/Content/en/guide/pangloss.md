# PanGloss

On Analyze texts, PanGloss runs the project's grammar on selected texts and compares its analyses with what FieldWorks stores.

PanGloss parses the project's grammar. Motif records parser warnings and refusals alongside the results.

For a FieldWorks Opinion, A means Approved, U means Unknown, and D means Disapproved. An empty dashed box in the FieldWorks Opinion position means no analysis is stored. In Analyze texts, ≠ with a solid underline marks a new or different PanGloss result; ∅ with a dashed underline means no parse, and ◖ with a dotted underline means a stopped search. These PanGloss cues are separate from the FieldWorks Opinion, and their glyphs and line styles carry meaning alongside color. Staged means a choice is waiting for Apply to FieldWorks project.
