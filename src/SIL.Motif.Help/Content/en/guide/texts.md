# Texts

**Texts** is where you choose material to measure and compare word analyses. Its tabs are **Matrix**, **Analyze texts**, **Lists**, and **What changed**. In **Analyze texts**, use **Read text** to follow words in context or **Word list** to inspect words directly. Choose texts or added words there, then use **Run assessment** to record results for that Selection.

The Matrix groups words by their stored project state and parser result. Select a cell to inspect its words, then open a word to see its FieldWorks analyses and parser readings. In the Matrix, available actions include **Approve**, **Disapprove**, **Make Unknown**, **Incorrect spelling**, and **Add as Unknown**. In **Analyze texts**, corresponding actions approve or disapprove a reading, make a reading Unknown, mark a spelling Incorrect, or add parser readings as Unknown. The action applies to the word form everywhere in the project, not only the occurrence currently shown.

Changes you collect remain pending until you review and apply them. The page’s **Review changes** link opens that list; it does not write anything to FieldWorks. [Text Coverage](term:text-coverage) appears on the Overview after a measurement.
