# Texts

**Texts** is where you choose material to measure and compare word analyses. Its tabs are **Matrix**, **Analyze texts**, **Lists**, and **What changed**. In **Analyze texts**, use **Read text** to follow words in context or **Word list** to inspect words directly. Choose texts or added words there, then use **Parse all words** to record results for that Selection.

The Matrix groups words by what FieldWorks holds, down the side, and what PanGloss found, across the top: **Same**, **Different**, **No parse**, **Stopped** or **Not parsed**. Each cell says in a few words what happened, such as **Kept**, **Lost** or **Built anyway**. It names both counts when words and places differ (for example, **4 words · 7 places**), and says **1 place** when the counts are the same. Point at a cell to read what its words have in common. The chips above the Matrix, such as **Lost** or **New: PanGloss proposes**, choose those cells for you.

Every word row uses the meaning named by its Matrix cell, including in **Lists** and **Analyze texts**. A card may show recorded comparison detail beneath that shared meaning. For example, a word with Unknown and Disapproved analyses can appear in **Unknown × Different** when PanGloss matched an analysis FieldWorks marked Disapproved; the row keeps the cell's **Differs: have a look** meaning and shows recorded comparison detail beneath it. When Approved and Disapproved analyses share a morphology, both opinions remain visible; that match does not establish which other analyses PanGloss matched.

An **Analyze texts** card can also show which FieldWorks item a grammar warning names in that word. **Other words that use -a** lists words with the same named item. Membership and spelling matches stay labelled as possible matches. This evidence records a connection to the warning; it does not say the warning caused the word's result. Open the warning to read PanGloss's full description.

A stopped search does not establish that an analysis was not built, even if it found some readings. Those words stay in **Stopped**, with **No result**; a word that was not parsed has no new comparison evidence. Matrix, the shared word lists and **Analyze texts** use the same per-word comparison.

**Word list** counts words by their FieldWorks opinion, with a separate filter for Approved, Unknown, Disapproved and Not in FieldWorks. These counts describe words, while each analysis in a word card keeps its own opinion. The shared row also shows the meaning, places and time. Identical forms and glosses can still name different morphemes; the list explains that identity difference beside the readings instead of treating spelling as a match.

Select a cell to list its words below the Matrix. The list's heading names the cell and says in one line what it means. When two or more of its words use the same morpheme, **What these words share** shows each such morpheme and how many of the words use it. Morphemes are matched by their identity in FieldWorks, never by spelling, and a shared one is often the first clue to a single cause. Choose **Open in text** to see a word in **Analyze texts**. Tick words to show **Add as Unknown** and **Incorrect spelling**. Approving, disapproving or making an analysis Unknown happens one analysis at a time, in **Analyze texts**. Each action applies to the word form everywhere in the project, not only the occurrence currently shown.

**Lists** holds the Matrix's named shortcuts, such as **Lost** or **Built something else**, with the same words, and opens on the first list that has words. One line names the list, counts its words and places, and says what it holds. Open a word to see FieldWorks' analysis over PanGloss's, lined up morpheme by morpheme, with the pieces that differ in blue and a sentence saying where the two part; **Try a Word** opens that word there. Tick words to hand off just those: the button says **AI Handoff for this word** or names the number of words. **AI Handoff for this list** sends the whole list, and an unticked-word button explains **Tick words first.** A list of stopped or unparsed words also offers **Parse again**. Opinions change one analysis at a time, in the text, never from a list.

Changes you collect remain pending until you review and apply them. The page’s **Review changes** link opens that list; it does not write anything to FieldWorks. [Text Coverage](term:text-coverage) appears on the Overview after a measurement.
