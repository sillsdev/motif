# Timing

Timing counts parser work in parser work, which do not change with how busy this computer is; compare them when judging a grammar change. **Timing** opens on **All** and shows recorded parser work for the current Selection. It reads timing saved by [Parse all words](term:parse-all-words); opening the page does not start a parse. If there is no timing for those words, choose **Parse again**. If **Stopped** or **Slowest** has no words, choose **Show all words** to return to the full Selection.

The **Words** row shows **All**, **Stopped**, and **Slowest** with their word counts. Choose **A Matrix cell** or **A list** to pick words from Texts; open **More** for words checked in Texts or words you enter by hand. **AI Handoff for these words** uses the current word group. **Re-run** opens the per-word time and parser work limits and the run controls. Its parser work limit starts at 20,000,000, and reaching it stops a word at the parser work limit. Shares use whole percentages; a positive share below one percent says **<1%**. **Parser** is the parser's own work that belongs to no single rule or lookup. **Not attributed** appears once in the kind legend for work the parser did not assign to a rule, a lookup or its own work; the kinds and **Not attributed** always add up to the total. The page shows work by kind and rule when the parser recorded it for every chosen word. A very large run keeps those as totals for the whole run, so they show only for all of its words together. A run stored before Motif kept those totals says the work was not kept and lists the rules without parser work or shares; choose **Parse all words** again to keep them. A chosen part of a very large run says the same without that advice, because parsing again does not keep work for a part. Open **Show calls** to see each kind's recorded call count; a kind that does not count calls says **Not counted**.

The compact word row names PanGloss's outcome as **Same**, **Different**, **No parse**, or **Stopped**. The meaning is separate and hidden in this list; a matching morphology alone does not imply agreement with a Disapproved opinion.

Select a rule row to see its costliest words; open **Inspect** on the row to see its FieldWorks facts and link. When object timing detail is limited, a rule's word list covers only the retained words, so a costlier use may be outside it. When a timing row names a lexical entry, it uses the entry's form and gloss when those facts are available. Long rule titles end with an ellipsis in the side panel; point at or focus the title to read it in full. While the Inspector is open, the rule table uses the available width and hides **Kind**. An amber warning mark appears only when a stored grammar finding names that same item by identity. Choose the mark to open that finding on the Warnings page. The inspector separates words that use a morpheme in a stored analysis from words where the parser tried it. Its word, parser work and call counts come from that item’s recorded timers; if none were recorded, it says so. In **Detailed statistics**, a word's parser work keep one shading scale across the selected words. **By word** uses those same words; other groups show the complete parse and say so. The groups for rules and other grammar objects show no parser work column, because the parser's object rows count a different unit; the rule table above gives each rule's parser work.

Choose **Slowest** to inspect timing for the words with the most parser work; each shows its parser work. To read individual word parser work, expand **Detailed statistics** and choose **By word**; Timing does not repeat those words in a separate list beside a rule's costliest words. To measure selected words again, open **Re-run**, choose **Seconds per word** or **Parser work limit**, then choose **Re-run chosen words**. The parser work limit starts at **20,000,000**. **Cancel** stops that rerun. When you retry, raise **Parser work limit** after a stop at the work limit or increase **Seconds per word** after a stop at the time safety net. When the current totals include re-runs, Timing labels the number of separate measurements. Open **Detailed statistics** to load its rows; changing the group loads the new group.

In **Detailed statistics**, **Stopped** counts words recorded as stopping at the work limit or at the time safety net, and the **Completion** column says which. For the work limit, raise **Parser work limit**; PanGloss also raises its derived work limit. The time safety net depends on how fast this computer is, so it is a time, not a count of work; increase **Seconds per word** for it. If the record does not distinguish the two, Timing keeps the reason general. **Analyses** counts words with more than one analysis; sort by that column to find them. A rule's share says how much of the work its steps recorded, not what caused a word to parse or stop.

A refused word wears **PanGloss can’t read it** and keeps the parser’s reason on its row and card. It is not a completed search that found no analysis; timing shows only measurements the parser recorded. Refused words are left out of **Re-run chosen words** because repeating them cannot produce a parse time.
