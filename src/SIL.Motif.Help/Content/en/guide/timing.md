# Timing

**Timing** shows where recorded parse time went: first by kind of rule, then by rule, then by word. It reads timing saved by [Parse all words](term:parse-all-words); opening the page does not start a parse. If there is no timing yet, choose **Parse all words** first.

Under **Look at**, choose **Step limit**, **Slowest**, or **All**. You can also use **Chosen in Texts**, a Matrix cell, a list, or words you enter by hand. The page shows the selected scope’s timing by kind and rule, followed by the slowest words. Select a rule to see the words that cost most under it.

For selected words that need another attempt, use the rerun controls to choose a longer per-word time limit or a larger step limit, then choose **Re-run chosen words**. **Cancel** stops that rerun. More time does not fix a search that reached its step limit; increase the step limit when that is the outcome.
