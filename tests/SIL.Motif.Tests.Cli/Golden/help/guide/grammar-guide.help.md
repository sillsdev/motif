# Grammar Guide

**Grammar Guide** lists the saved Guide sections for the open project as parts and sections, the structured explanation of your grammar. Read it here beside your texts, and edit its writing here when you want to. The grammar itself, such as rules and categories, is read and edited only in FieldWorks.

## Parts and sections

The outline on the left lists the parts of the guide, each with the sections filed under it, exactly as the records are arranged in FieldWorks. Select a section to read its discussion. **On This Page** lists the headings in that section's text.

## Reading and editing

A section opens for reading. Choose **Edit** to type in it, with the project's own paragraph styles, character styles and writing systems, and **Done** to go back to reading. Bold, italic and underline, links to other sections, links to any object by its project path, and **Open as Markdown…** for the whole section are on the tool bar while you edit.

What you type is added to your pending changes. It does not change the FieldWorks project until you choose **Apply Pending Changes**. That button applies every pending change in the project, not only this section's, so review them first in [Pending Changes](guide:pending-changes). Your typing stays safe when Motif reads a newer Baseline, and **Compare…** shows how your edit differs from a section that was also changed in FieldWorks.

## In the Grammar

**In the Grammar** lists the names of the grammar objects the section's text links to, each with **Open in FieldWorks**. A link whose object was deleted is listed as missing. A link to an object outside this project is listed as outside it.

## Examples from your texts

Examples from your texts, counts of approved readings and the Proposals that disagree about a section appear only when Motif can match them to the section's linked grammar. Until then this part of the page is not shown, and Motif does not guess from similar words.
