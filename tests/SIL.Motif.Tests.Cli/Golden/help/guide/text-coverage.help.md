# Text Coverage

[Text Coverage](term:text-coverage) measures how much of the Default Selection produced a completed parse in the saved results. The [Overview command](cmd:overview) reports selected words and their places separately: one written form can appear many times in a Text.

Parse completion is not human approval. It does not establish that a person kept an analysis at each place. Progress does not use this measure to fill a chapter approval strip or calculate an approval percentage.

A completed search with no analysis differs from an incomplete search that stopped at a work limit or the time safety net. Missing, unreadable or stale results do not mean zero. Digit-only places and places in other writing systems are counted separately where the stored Text summaries provide those counts.

The command can also group recorded parse coverage by frequency band. A word list's supplied counts describe that list, not places in a Text. Choose **Texts** to inspect the current Selection's words and places, and see [Parse all words](term:parse-all-words) for the stored run's scope. See [Progress](guide:progress) for the window's Next rows and health details.
