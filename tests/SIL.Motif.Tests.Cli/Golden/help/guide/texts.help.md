# Texts

**Texts** shows the reader and the current Selection in **Sources** and **Passage**. Open the side panel to inspect Paratext Source metadata or choose **FieldWorks Texts**. Check the FieldWorks Texts to include in the current Selection, then choose a Text in the reader to open its Passage and available analyses. Opening Texts does not start a parse.

Selecting a Paratext Source shows its saved Context, local folder and Current chapter. External Passage text is unavailable; Motif does not substitute a FieldWorks Text with the same name. See [Sources](guide:sources) for the supported kinds and the difference between Source, Context and Current.

Choose a word to inspect its recorded analysis and available actions, and to see every place the word occurs in the word panel; see [Passage](guide:passage). To inspect the word rows for the whole Context, open [Words](guide:words). To save the material measured for future runs, configure a [Default Selection](guide:default-selection).
