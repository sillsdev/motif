# Words

**Words** lists every written shape in the whole Context: all the chapters or documents you have chosen to work on, and none of the ones left for Later. Choosing a different Current chapter in [Texts](guide:texts) does not change this list or its counts.

## One row for each written shape

A spelling that occurs in many places has one row. The row counts every place it occurs, in every chosen Text. Spellings are matched after the same Unicode normalisation Motif already uses, so a word typed with a combining accent and the same word typed with a single accented letter are one row. Two writing systems that happen to spell a word alike are never joined: each keeps its own row and its own font and direction.

Motif does not work out stems, families of related words or sound patterns here. If the same spelling has different analyses in different places, the row says how many readings it has, and each reading stays separate when you keep one.

When the parser has read a word, its row shows how many analyses it built, for example 2 analyses.

## Opening a word

Choose a row to open its word card and, beside it, the list of places it occurs. This is the same panel [Passage](guide:passage) uses. Tick the places you mean, pick the reading to keep, then choose **Approve**. Only the ticked places are staged, and nothing reaches FieldWorks until you choose **Apply**. **Not a Word** marks the spelling as not a word for the whole project.

The filter chips narrow the rows by where the project stands, and the search box narrows them by spelling. The rows load as you scroll, so a long list stays quick.

## Sources that are not FieldWorks Texts

This page reads the FieldWorks Texts in your Context. Words from a Paratext project, a text file or a JSONL file are not listed until Motif can read their words with the same exact places; they are not hidden under another count.

## Keys

Up and Down move between rows, Page Up and Page Down move a screen, Home and End go to the first and last row, and Enter or Space opens or closes the word card. A, D and U stage an opinion for the focused analysis. [Keyboard Shortcuts](guide:keyboard-shortcuts) lists them all.
