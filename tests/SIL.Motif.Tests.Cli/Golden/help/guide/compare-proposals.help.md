# Compare Proposals

Check two Proposals in **Proposals**, then choose **Compare 2 Proposals**. The comparison shows exactly that pair side by side. Choose **Back to Proposals** to return to the open documents; your edits and checks are still there.

Each side shows the Proposal's name, status, Rationale, the changes it makes to the grammar and its Comments count. **Will Fill In** and **Will Change** list the words each Proposal affects. **Where They Differ** lists the words whose effect differs between the two, with **Show in Texts** for each.

Motif compares two Proposals only when it can show they were measured the same way: the same project, baseline grammar, Text, decisions, parser and tokenizer, with a complete search for every listed word. When any of that is missing or has changed, the comparison says **Outdated** or **Comparison Unavailable**, gives the reason and offers **Measure Both Again**. Motif never subtracts two measurements it cannot match, and an unmeasured or incomplete word is never shown as no change.

Two Proposals that change some of the same words overlap. Overlap alone does not show which is better. Evidence covers a single Text, and the exact approved places a Proposal stops filling in are not available, so the comparison does not list them.

Applying one Proposal marks the other out of date until its evidence is refreshed. Each side has its own **Apply…** and follows the usual confirmation. See [Proposals](guide:proposals), [Review a Proposal](guide:review-a-proposal) and [Apply to FieldWorks](guide:apply-to-fieldworks).
