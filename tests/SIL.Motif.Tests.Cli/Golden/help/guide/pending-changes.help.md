# Pending Changes

Changes staged from a word stay pending until a person applies them. Open **Proposals** to inspect the pending document and its Comments and evidence. Staging does not write changes to FieldWorks.

## Keeps staged from the Passage

Keeping a reading in **Texts**, whether with Enter, Shift+Enter or **Approve** in the word panel, stages one pending change for each place. Each change records the word, the place it belongs to and the reading you chose, and a place shows as staged only after its change has been written. Keeping the same reading at a place that is already staged does nothing, so nothing is staged twice.

A keep of a reading that only the parser offered is a new analysis. FieldWorks creates it only when you choose **Apply**, and it is still your decision until then. Before applying, open the pending document in [Proposals](guide:proposals), which lists the staged changes by Text. Proposals has no button to undo one; to take back the last staged choice, press Ctrl+Z (⌘+Z on macOS) in the window.

If a keep is refused, the sentence beside the word panel or in the status line says why, and no place that was not written is shown as staged.

**Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y, ⌘+Z and ⌘+Shift+Z on macOS) affect staged changes only, one staging action at a time, and the window has no on-screen Undo button. Before applying, check that the changes still fit the project and explicitly measure what they do. Missing or outdated evidence is not a successful check. See [Apply to FieldWorks](guide:apply-to-fieldworks) and [When a Change No Longer Fits](guide:when-a-change-no-longer-fits).
