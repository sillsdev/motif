# Read Trial progress

Waits for a Trial job and reads its state. When the job completes, `motif trial --wait` prints the Trial's review summary; with `--json` it is the same summary `motif_trial` returns, with a `gate` that a script can exit on.

| Gate | What it means | Exit code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `green` | No unexpected loss, blocking incomplete evidence or review hold remains; every declared required case was measured. | 0 |
| `loss` | A measured word went from analysed to no analysis, or lost a confirmed reading, and the word was not listed as an expected loss. A loss wins over `unmeasured`. | 6 |
| `unmeasured` | The Trial measured no words, left required cases unmeasured, was interrupted, did not finish (for example the parser was killed), or stopped a word by budget or timeout that had an analysis before the change. A new second reading held for review, or a declared gain/Parsimony hold, also prevents green. | 2 |

A word that was stopped but had no analysis before the change does not change the gate. The gate names stopped words that had a Baseline analysis under `stoppedWords`, expected losses under `expectedLosses` and unlisted losses under `unexpectedLosses`. The report also keeps every stopped word in `report.stoppedWords`, including words with no Baseline analysis: each names its before or after side, reason codes and specific parser explanation. For `invalid-evidence`, the explanation identifies the invalid shape or morphology evidence. These diagnostics remain available when the optional detail index is not retained.

The gate reads the Trial's retained evidence, so `--review-set none` does not hide a loss. `--expect-loss <file>` names words, one per line (blank lines are ignored, and a word matches whatever its Unicode normalisation), that the change intends to lose, such as retirements; it needs `--wait`. Listed words do not make the gate a loss; any other lost word still fails. The summary prints whatever the gate. Read the saved report later with [Read a Trial report](cmd:proposal%20trial).

If PanGloss is killed by a signal or terminated by the operating system, the Job is `failed` with `failureCategory` `parser-killed` and `failureCode` `assess.parser-killed`. The waited command exits 2, even when the parser wrote partial or complete-looking output. If there is memory evidence, the category is `out-of-memory` and the code is `assess.out-of-memory` instead. A completed Dry Run and retained partial review evidence remain available; the failed run does not publish a completed Trial.

With `--json`, a terminal Job that could not finish the Trial is printed as one JSON object on stdout, with its status and failure category. Warnings and diagnostics go to stderr. An enqueue refusal is a FailureEnvelope on stderr.

The stored result keeps its `gate` and `report.losses`: `newNoParseWords` and `newApprovedLossWords` name every affected word, and `newlyLostReadings` names each word and frozen reading identity. These lists survive when the optional detail index exceeds the store budget. For each word listed in `--expect-loss`, `newlyLostReadings` also carries one row per lost Reading with its canonical `signature` (ordered morphemes), the lost copy `count` and `remainingReadingCount` (lexicon-backed Readings still emitted); a word that lost every Reading lists them all with zero remaining. `losses.retirementAttestation` is bound to the report digest and its `complete` is true only when those rows cover the full retirement multiset of the waived words across every measured case; `limitations` says why not. The saved report keeps every retirement row; a read returns the rows filled to a 16 KB bound and `retirementAttestation.page`, `pageCount` and `rowCount` say how many exist, and `--page <N>` reads the others (`--report` pins the exact saved report). Nothing is refused for size. Rows with a `signature` (the retirement multiset) and rows without one (confirmed losses of frozen expectations) describe the same loss from two sides: a gate must read each kind on its own and must not sum across the two kinds. Expected losses are frozen when the Trial is queued; later reads keep that Trial’s verdict.

When a word had a Baseline analysis but its spelling cannot be segmented by the candidate phoneme inventory, the parser may mark it `skipped`. This is a named loss, including any confirmed readings it lost, rather than a pass or an unknown search result. Malformed evidence cannot establish that loss. A listed intended inventory loss is covered by the Trial's saved expected losses.

`report.regressions` names up to 10 lost or regressed words per cause with their analyses before and after, the count of the rest and a `nextStep` to page them; see [Trial a Proposal](cmd:trial). The printed `Unexpected losses` list stops at 10 words (`--json` lists all).

Sets required by `[gate] word-sets` are measured even with `--review-set none`; [config show](cmd:config%20show) documents the declaration. Held second readings are named in `report.heldWords`, independently of the detail budget. Independently approve the added reading and run a fresh Trial to clear that hold. Older reports without compact gate facts are unmeasured: use `proposal trial --refresh-report` with retained inputs, or rerun the Trial.

Add `--wait-terminal` to cancel the exact Trial at the wait deadline and return after its execution and parser processes have stopped. The `job.wait-timeout` refusal reports the terminal job `status` and `jobId`. With an ordinary wait, the Trial can keep running after the wait expires.
